In July 2025, the ratio of total business inventories to sales was 1.37, compared to 1.38 in June 2025, 1.40 in July 2024, and 1.43 in February 2020.

The inflation-adjusted version from BEA shows inventories at 1.54 times sales in July 2025, following a ratio of 1.55 in June 2025, and 1.57 one year prior, in July 2024. In 2019, real monthly inventories were 1.55 times real monthly sales, on average.
