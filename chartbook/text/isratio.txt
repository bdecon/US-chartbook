In March 2025, the ratio of total business inventories to sales was 1.34, compared to 1.35 in February 2025, 1.37 in March 2024, and 1.43 in February 2020.

The inflation-adjusted version from BEA shows inventories at 1.51 times sales in February 2025, following a ratio of 1.53 in January 2025, and 1.54 one year prior, in February 2024. In 2019, real monthly inventories were 1.55 times real monthly sales, on average.
