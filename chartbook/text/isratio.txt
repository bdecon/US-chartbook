In August 2026, the ratio of total business inventories to sales was 1.33, compared to 1.34 in July 2026, 1.40 in August 2025, and 1.43 in February 2020.

The inflation-adjusted version from BEA shows inventories at 1.52 times sales in December 2025, following a ratio of 1.54 in November 2025, and 1.55 one year prior, in December 2024. In 2019, real monthly inventories were 1.55 times real monthly sales, on average.
