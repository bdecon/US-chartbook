For production and nonsupervisory workers, the highest average hourly earnings in April 2025 are in the utilities industry (\$45.89), followed by the information industry (\$42.86), and the mining and logging industry (\$37.09). The lowest wage industries in the latest data, by average hourly earnings, are leisure and hospitality (\$20.02) and retail trade (\$21.44).

Over the past year, 10 of the 12 industry groups have wage growth above the increase in prices indicated by the consumer price index (see {\color{orange!50!yellow}\textbf{---}}). The information industry had the fastest nominal growth rate, at 5.6 percent, followed by 4.8 percent in professional and business services and 4.7 percent in manufacturing.
