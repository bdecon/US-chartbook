For production and nonsupervisory workers, the highest average hourly earnings in May 2026 are in the utilities industry (\$48.05), followed by the information industry (\$45.47), and the construction industry (\$38.97). The lowest wage industries in the latest data, by average hourly earnings, are leisure and hospitality (\$20.96) and retail trade (\$22.24).

Over the past year, six of the 12 industry groups have wage growth above the increase in prices indicated by the consumer price index (see {\color{orange!50!yellow}\textbf{---}}). The information industry had the fastest nominal growth rate, at 6.1 percent, followed by 5.4 percent in utilities and 5.0 percent in construction.
