For production and nonsupervisory workers, the highest average hourly earnings in February 2026 are in the utilities industry (\$47.92), followed by the information industry (\$44.73), and the financial activities industry (\$38.62). The lowest wage industries in the latest data, by average hourly earnings, are leisure and hospitality (\$20.85) and retail trade (\$22.03).

Over the past year, 11 of the 12 industry groups have wage growth above the increase in prices indicated by the consumer price index (see {\color{orange!50!yellow}\textbf{---}}). The information industry had the fastest nominal growth rate, at 5.8 percent, followed by 5.2 percent in financial activities and 5.1 percent in construction.
