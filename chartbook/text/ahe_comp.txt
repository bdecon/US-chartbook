For production and nonsupervisory workers, the highest average hourly earnings in September 2026 are in the utilities industry (\$49.49), followed by the information industry (\$45.95), and the financial activities industry (\$39.21). The lowest wage industries in the latest data, by average hourly earnings, are leisure and hospitality (\$21.34) and retail trade (\$22.63).

Over the past year, eight of the 12 industry groups have wage growth above the increase in prices indicated by the consumer price index (see {\color{orange!50!yellow}\textbf{---}}). The utilities industry had the fastest nominal growth rate, at 6.3 percent, followed by 5.1 percent in information and 4.9 percent in leisure and hospitality.
