For production and nonsupervisory workers, the highest average hourly earnings in August 2026 are in the utilities industry (\$49.94), followed by the information industry (\$45.46), and the construction industry (\$39.36). The lowest wage industries in the latest data, by average hourly earnings, are leisure and hospitality (\$21.22) and retail trade (\$22.41).

Over the past year, nine of the 12 industry groups have wage growth above the increase in prices indicated by the consumer price index (see {\color{orange!50!yellow}\textbf{---}}). The utilities industry had the fastest nominal growth rate, at 7.8 percent, followed by 5.0 percent in construction and 4.7 percent in leisure and hospitality.
