For production and nonsupervisory workers, the highest average hourly earnings in June 2025 are in the utilities industry (\$45.93), followed by the information industry (\$42.73), and the mining and logging industry (\$37.67). The lowest wage industries in the latest data, by average hourly earnings, are leisure and hospitality (\$20.20) and retail trade (\$21.43).

Over the past year, nine of the 12 industry groups have wage growth above the increase in prices indicated by the consumer price index (see {\color{orange!50!yellow}\textbf{---}}). The professional and business services industry had the fastest nominal growth rate, at 5.0 percent, followed by 4.9 percent in information and 4.6 percent in construction.
