For production and nonsupervisory workers, the highest average hourly earnings in April 2026 are in the utilities industry (\$48.11), followed by the information industry (\$45.43), and the financial activities industry (\$38.79). The lowest wage industries in the latest data, by average hourly earnings, are leisure and hospitality (\$20.93) and retail trade (\$22.12).

Over the past year, 10 of the 12 industry groups have wage growth above the increase in prices indicated by the consumer price index (see {\color{orange!50!yellow}\textbf{---}}). The information industry had the fastest nominal growth rate, at 6.6 percent, followed by 5.2 percent in utilities and 4.9 percent in financial activities.
