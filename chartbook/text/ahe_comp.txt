For production and nonsupervisory workers, the highest average hourly earnings in August 2025 are in the utilities industry (\$46.36), followed by the information industry (\$43.49), and the financial activities industry (\$37.71). The lowest wage industries in the latest data, by average hourly earnings, are leisure and hospitality (\$20.31) and retail trade (\$21.66).

Over the past year, 11 of the 12 industry groups have wage growth above the increase in prices indicated by the consumer price index (see {\color{orange!50!yellow}\textbf{---}}). The information industry had the fastest nominal growth rate, at 6.7 percent, followed by 4.8 percent in financial activities and 4.3 percent in construction.
