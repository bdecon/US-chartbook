For production and nonsupervisory workers, the highest average hourly earnings in September 2025 are in the utilities industry (\$46.56), followed by the information industry (\$43.68), and the financial activities industry (\$37.70). The lowest wage industries in the latest data, by average hourly earnings, are leisure and hospitality (\$20.39) and retail trade (\$21.72).

Over the past year, 10 of the 12 industry groups have wage growth above the increase in prices indicated by the consumer price index (see {\color{orange!50!yellow}\textbf{---}}). The information industry had the fastest nominal growth rate, at 7.1 percent, followed by 4.7 percent in wholesale trade and 4.4 percent in manufacturing.
