In August 2025, the perceived likelihood of leaving one's job voluntarily in the next 12 months is 18.9 percent, compared to 21 percent in 2019 (see {\color{violet!50!blue}\textbf{---}}). In the latest month, the perceived probability losing one's job is 14.5 percent, compared to 14.3 percent in 2019 (see {\color{cyan}\textbf{---}}).

During the pandemic, in April 2020, job loss expectations exceeded job leaving expectations. In August 2025, job leaving expectations exceed job loss expectations by 4.5 percentage points, compared to 6.8 points in 2019.
