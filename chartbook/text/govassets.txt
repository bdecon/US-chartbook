In the fourth quarter of 2025, the market value of government assets, excluding land, is \$33.3 trillion, equivalent to 105.8 percent of GDP. Of this, state and local government nonfinancial assets, such as buildings and equipment, are equivalent to 55.2 percent of GDP \seebox{cyan!36!white}, and state and local government financial assets, such as insurance trust funds, equate to 16.5 percent of GDP \seebox{blue!75!cyan}.

The market value of federal government nonfinancial assets is equivalent to 15.7 percent of GDP in 2025 Q4 \seebox{violet!60!white}. Federal government financial assets are valued at 18.4 percent of GDP \seebox{blue!28!violet!90!black}.
