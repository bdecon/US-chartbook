In the second quarter of 2025, the market value of government assets, excluding land, is \$31.8 trillion, equivalent to 104.3 percent of GDP. Of this, state and local government nonfinancial assets, such as buildings and equipment, are equivalent to 56.0 percent of GDP \seebox{cyan!36!white}, and state and local government financial assets, such as insurance trust funds, equate to 17.3 percent of GDP \seebox{blue!75!cyan}.

The market value of federal government nonfinancial assets is equivalent to 16.0 percent of GDP in 2025 Q2 \seebox{violet!60!white}. Federal government financial assets are valued at 14.9 percent of GDP \seebox{blue!28!violet!90!black}.
