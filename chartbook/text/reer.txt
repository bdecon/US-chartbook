The Bank for International Settlements (BIS) \href{https://www.bis.org/statistics/eer.htm}{calculates} \textbf{real effective exchange rates} for many countries. As of July 2025, the US dollar real effective exchange rate has increased eight percent since 2020, and decreased 2.1 percent over the past year. In 2019, the index average was 98.6. The average over the past three months is 108.9.
