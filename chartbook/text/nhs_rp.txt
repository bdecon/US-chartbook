The Census Bureau also tracks the \textbf{sales price} of new single-family homes. In October 2025, the median new home sold for \$392,300 (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}), and the average sales price was \$498,000. The inflation-adjusted median sales price has decreased 10.5 percent over the past year, and decreased six percent over the past five years. Since 1989, the inflation-adjusted median new home sales price increased 29.6 percent.
