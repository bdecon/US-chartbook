The Census Bureau also tracks the \textbf{sales price} of new single-family homes. In August 2026, the median new home sold for \$393,700 (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}), and the average sales price was \$478,700. The inflation-adjusted median sales price has decreased 8.9 percent over the past year, and decreased 18.2 percent over the past three years. Since 1989, the inflation-adjusted median new home sales price increased 26.0 percent.
