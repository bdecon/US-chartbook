The Census Bureau also tracks the \textbf{sales price} of new single-family homes. In March 2026, the median new home sold for \$387,400 (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}), and the average sales price was \$503,100. The inflation-adjusted median sales price has decreased 9.1 percent over the past year, and decreased 19.3 percent over the past three years. Since 1989, the inflation-adjusted median new home sales price increased 25.7 percent.
