The Census Bureau also tracks the \textbf{sales price} of new single-family homes. In May 2025, the median new home sold for \$426,600 (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}), and the average sales price was \$522,200. The inflation-adjusted median sales price has increased 0.6 percent over the past year, and increased 7.7 percent over the past five years. Since 1989, the inflation-adjusted median new home sales price increased 42.2 percent.
