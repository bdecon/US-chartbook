The Census Bureau also tracks the \textbf{sales price} of new single-family homes. In January 2026, the median new home sold for \$400,500 (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}), and the average sales price was \$499,500. The inflation-adjusted median sales price has decreased 8.9 percent over the past year, and decreased 9.2 percent over the past five years. Since 1989, the inflation-adjusted median new home sales price increased 32.0 percent.
