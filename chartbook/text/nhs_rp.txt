The Census Bureau also tracks the \textbf{sales price} of new single-family homes. In December 2025, the median new home sold for \$414,400 (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}), and the average sales price was \$532,600. The inflation-adjusted median sales price has decreased 4.6 percent over the past year, and decreased 1.8 percent over the past five years. Since 1989, the inflation-adjusted median new home sales price increased 37.0 percent.
