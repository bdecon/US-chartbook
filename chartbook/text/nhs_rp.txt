The Census Bureau also tracks the \textbf{sales price} of new single-family homes. In August 2025, the median new home sold for \$413,500 (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}), and the average sales price was \$534,100. The inflation-adjusted median sales price has decreased one percent over the past year, and increased 3.2 percent over the past five years. Since 1989, the inflation-adjusted median new home sales price increased 36.8 percent.
