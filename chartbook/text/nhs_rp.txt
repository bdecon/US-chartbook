The Census Bureau also tracks the \textbf{sales price} of new single-family homes. In March 2025, the median new home sold for \$403,600 (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}), and the average sales price was \$497,700. The inflation-adjusted median sales price has decreased 9.7 percent over the past year, and decreased 0.7 percent over the past five years. Since 1989, the inflation-adjusted median new home sales price increased 35.3 percent.
