The Census Bureau also tracks the \textbf{sales price} of new single-family homes. In April 2026, the median new home sold for \$422,500 (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}), and the average sales price was \$508,800. The inflation-adjusted median sales price has decreased 1.6 percent over the past year, and decreased 7.7 percent over the past three years. Since 1989, the inflation-adjusted median new home sales price increased 36.0 percent.
