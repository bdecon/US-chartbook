The Census Bureau also tracks the \textbf{sales price} of new single-family homes. In June 2025, the median new home sold for \$401,800 (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}), and the average sales price was \$501,000. The inflation-adjusted median sales price has decreased 5.5 percent over the past year, and decreased 1.3 percent over the past five years. Since 1989, the inflation-adjusted median new home sales price increased 33.5 percent.
