The Census Bureau also tracks the \textbf{sales price} of new single-family homes. In July 2025, the median new home sold for \$403,800 (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}), and the average sales price was \$487,300. The inflation-adjusted median sales price has decreased 8.4 percent over the past year, and increased 1.1 percent over the past five years. Since 1989, the inflation-adjusted median new home sales price increased 34.0 percent.
