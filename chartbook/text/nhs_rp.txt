The Census Bureau also tracks the \textbf{sales price} of new single-family homes. In July 2026, the median new home sold for \$393,800 (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}), and the average sales price was \$508,800. The inflation-adjusted median sales price has decreased 4.1 percent over the past year, and decreased 17.3 percent over the past three years. Since 1989, the inflation-adjusted median new home sales price increased 26.4 percent.
