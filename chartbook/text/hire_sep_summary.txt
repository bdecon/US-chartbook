Flows into and out of employment provide an overview of labor force flows. Each month, employers hire new workers while some existing workers leave jobs. The August 2025 Job Openings and Labor Turnover Survey (JOLTS) \href{https://www.bls.gov/jlt/}{shows} a three-month average of 5.2 million new hires (see \colordashline{violet!60!black}) and 5.2 million job separations (see \tcolorline{violet!60!blue!50!white}), compared to 5.3 million hires and 5.2 million separations in August 2024.

The Current Population Survey (CPS), which includes self-employment among other differences, \href{https://www.bls.gov/jlt/}{shows} 6.4 million newly employed people (see \scolorline{blue!70!green!90!white}) in August 2025, and 6.4 million job leavers (see \colordotline{green!60!black}). One year prior, in August 2024, there were 6.2 million newly employed and 6.2 million job leavers.
