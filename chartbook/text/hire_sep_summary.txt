Flows into and out of employment provide an overview of labor force flows. Each month, employers hire new workers while some existing workers leave jobs. The January 2026 Job Openings and Labor Turnover Survey (JOLTS) \href{https://www.bls.gov/jlt/}{shows} a three-month average of 5.2 million new hires (see \colordashline{violet!60!black}) and 5.1 million job separations (see \tcolorline{violet!60!blue!50!white}), compared to 5.2 million hires and 5.1 million separations in January 2025.

The Current Population Survey (CPS), which includes self-employment among other differences, \href{https://www.bls.gov/jlt/}{shows} 6.3 million newly employed people (see \scolorline{blue!70!green!90!white}) in February 2026, and 6.4 million job leavers (see \colordotline{forestgreen}). One year prior, in February 2025, there were 6.1 million newly employed and 6.0 million job leavers.
