FOMC meeting participants provide \href{https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm}{projections} which can be used to summarize policymaker views on the future path of the federal funds rate, as seen by the people who set it. As of September 16, 2026, the median projected federal funds rate is 4.1 percent for year-end 2026, 4.1 percent for 2027, and 3.9 percent for 2028 \seebox{purple}.
