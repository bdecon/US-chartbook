FOMC meeting participants provide \href{https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm}{projections} which can be used to summarize policymaker views on the future path of the federal funds rate, as seen by the people who set it. As of September 17, 2025, the median projected federal funds rate is 3.6 percent for year-end 2025, 3.4 percent for 2026, and 3.1 percent for 2027 \seebox{purple}.
