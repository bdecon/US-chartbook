As of September 24, 2025, the \textbf{year-to-date total return} for the S\&P 500 is 12.9 percent. The communication services sector returned 21.3 percent, the information technology sector returned 19.8 percent, and the industrials sector gained 15.5 percent. The largest loss is in the health care sector, with a total decrease of 0.8 percent.
