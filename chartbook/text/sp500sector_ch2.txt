As of September 8, 2025, the \textbf{year-to-date total return} for the S\&P 500 is 10.4 percent. The communication services sector returned 18.6 percent, the industrials sector returned 14.3 percent, and the information technology sector gained 13.7 percent. The largest loss is in the health care sector, with a total decrease of 0.2 percent.
