As of May 16, 2025, the \textbf{year-to-date total return} for the S\&P 500 is 1.3 percent. The industrials sector returned 8.9 percent, the utilities sector returned 8.2 percent, and the financials sector gained 6.6 percent. The largest loss is in the health care sector, with a total decrease of 3.5 percent.
