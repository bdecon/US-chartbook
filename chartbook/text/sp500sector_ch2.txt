As of July 14, 2025, the \textbf{year-to-date total return} for the S\&P 500 is 6.6 percent. The industrials sector returned 14.6 percent, the communication services sector returned 10.6 percent, and the information technology sector gained 9.9 percent. The largest loss is in the health care sector, with a total decrease of 1.9 percent.
