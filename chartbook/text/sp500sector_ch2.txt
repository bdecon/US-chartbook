As of August 1, 2025, the \textbf{year-to-date total return} for the S\&P 500 is 6.1 percent. The industrials sector returned 13.6 percent, the utilities sector returned 13.2 percent, and the information technology sector gained 10.6 percent. The largest loss is in the health care sector, with a total decrease of 4.8 percent.
