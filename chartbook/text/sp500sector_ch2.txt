As of July 28, 2025, the \textbf{year-to-date total return} for the S\&P 500 is 8.6 percent. The industrials sector returned 17.2 percent, the information technology sector returned 13.6 percent, and the communication services sector gained 10.6 percent. The largest loss is in the health care sector, with a total decrease of 1.8 percent.
