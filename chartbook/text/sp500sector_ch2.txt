As of August 4, 2025, the \textbf{year-to-date total return} for the S\&P 500 is 7.6 percent. The utilities sector returned 15.1 percent, the industrials sector returned 14.6 percent, and the information technology sector gained 12.8 percent. The largest loss is in the health care sector, with a total decrease of 3.5 percent.
