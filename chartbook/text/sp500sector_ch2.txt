As of August 12, 2025, the \textbf{year-to-date total return} for the S\&P 500 is 9.6 percent. The information technology sector returned 15.3 percent, the industrials sector returned 15.2 percent, and the communication services sector gained 13.9 percent. The largest loss is in the health care sector, with a total decrease of 4.8 percent.
