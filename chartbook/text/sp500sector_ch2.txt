As of August 13, 2025, the \textbf{year-to-date total return} for the S\&P 500 is 9.9 percent. The industrials sector returned 15.6 percent, the information technology sector returned 15.3 percent, and the communication services sector gained 14.8 percent. The largest loss is in the health care sector, with a total decrease of 3.3 percent.
