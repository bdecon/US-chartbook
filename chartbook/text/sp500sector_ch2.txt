As of July 30, 2025, the \textbf{year-to-date total return} for the S\&P 500 is 8.2 percent. The industrials sector returned 15.3 percent, the information technology sector returned 13.8 percent, and the utilities sector gained 12.4 percent. The largest loss is in the health care sector, with a total decrease of 2.7 percent.
