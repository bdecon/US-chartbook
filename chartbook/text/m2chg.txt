The M2 money stock increased 1.4 percent in March 2025, over the previous month, following a decrease of 0.1 percent in February, and virtually no change in January. Over the past 12 months, the money stock increased 4.2 percent (see {\color{green!80!blue}\textbf{---}}). The M2 money stock has increased 36.4 percent, in total, over the past five years.
