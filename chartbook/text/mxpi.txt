The Bureau of Labor Statistics \href{https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ximpim.nr0.htm}{report} changes in the prices of imports and exports. Over the year ending April 2025, \textbf{US import prices} were unchanged (see {\color{cyan!58!white}\textbf{---}}), following increases of 0.8 percent in March and 1.7 percent in February. Excluding fuels, US import prices increased 1.2 percent in April 2025 and grew 1.4 percent in March. In 2019, US import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.3 percent. Excluding fuels, import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.1 percent in 2019.

\textbf{Prices of US exports} grew two percent over the year ending April 2025 (see {\color{blue!90!black}\textbf{---}}), following increases of 2.6 percent in March, and 2.6 percent in February. In 2019, export prices decreased at an average rate of 0.8 percent.
