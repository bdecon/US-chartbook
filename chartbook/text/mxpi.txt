The Bureau of Labor Statistics \href{https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ximpim.nr0.htm}{reports} changes in the prices of imports and exports. Over the year ending May 2026, \textbf{US import prices} grew 6.7 percent (see {\color{paleblue}\textbf{---}}), following increases of 4.2 percent in April and 2.3 percent in March. Excluding fuels, US import prices increased 3.7 percent in May 2026 and grew 2.8 percent in April. In 2019, US import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.3 percent. Excluding fuels, import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.1 percent in 2019.

\textbf{Prices of US exports} grew 11.2 percent over the year ending May 2026 (see {\color{blue!90!black}\textbf{---}}), following increases of 9.1 percent in April and 5.5 percent in March. In 2019, export prices decreased at an average rate of 0.8 percent.
