The Bureau of Labor Statistics \href{https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ximpim.nr0.htm}{report} changes in the prices of imports and exports. Over the year ending May 2025, \textbf{US import prices} grew 0.2 percent (see {\color{cyan!58!white}\textbf{---}}), following virtually no change in April and 0.8 percent in March. Excluding fuels, US import prices increased 1.7 percent in May 2025 and grew 1.2 percent in April. In 2019, US import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.3 percent. Excluding fuels, import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.1 percent in 2019.

\textbf{Prices of US exports} grew 1.7 percent over the year ending May 2025 (see {\color{blue!90!black}\textbf{---}}), following increases of 1.9 percent in April, and 2.5 percent in March. In 2019, export prices decreased at an average rate of 0.8 percent.
