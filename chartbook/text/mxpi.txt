The Bureau of Labor Statistics \href{https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ximpim.nr0.htm}{report} changes in the prices of imports and exports. Over the year ending July 2025, \textbf{US import prices} fell 0.2 percent (see {\color{cyan!58!white}\textbf{---}}), following decreases of 0.5 percent in June and 0.3 percent in May. Excluding fuels, US import prices increased 0.9 percent in July 2025 and grew 0.8 percent in June. In 2019, US import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.3 percent. Excluding fuels, import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.1 percent in 2019.

\textbf{Prices of US exports} grew 2.2 percent over the year ending July 2025 (see {\color{blue!90!black}\textbf{---}}), following increases of 2.6 percent in June, and 1.8 percent in May. In 2019, export prices decreased at an average rate of 0.8 percent.
