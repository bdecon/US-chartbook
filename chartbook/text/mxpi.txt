The Bureau of Labor Statistics \href{https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ximpim.nr0.htm}{reports} changes in the prices of imports and exports. Over the year ending August 2026, \textbf{US import prices} grew seven percent (see {\color{paleblue}\textbf{---}}), following increases of 6.1 percent in July and 6.7 percent in June. Excluding fuels, US import prices increased 5.5 percent in August 2026 and grew 4.5 percent in July. In 2019, US import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.3 percent. Excluding fuels, import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.1 percent in 2019.

\textbf{Prices of US exports} grew 8.6 percent over the year ending August 2026 (see {\color{blue!90!black}\textbf{---}}), following increases of 8.1 percent in July and ten percent in June. In 2019, export prices decreased at an average rate of 0.8 percent.
