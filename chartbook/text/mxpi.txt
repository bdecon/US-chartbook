The Bureau of Labor Statistics \href{https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ximpim.nr0.htm}{reports} changes in the prices of imports and exports. Over the year ending February 2026, \textbf{US import prices} grew 1.3 percent (see {\color{paleblue}\textbf{---}}), following an increase of 0.3 percent in January and virtually no change in December 2025. Excluding fuels, US import prices increased 2.5 percent in February 2026 and grew 1.5 percent in January. In 2019, US import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.3 percent. Excluding fuels, import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.1 percent in 2019.

\textbf{Prices of US exports} grew 3.5 percent over the year ending February 2026 (see {\color{blue!90!black}\textbf{---}}), following increases of 2.6 percent in January, and 3.4 percent in December 2025. In 2019, export prices decreased at an average rate of 0.8 percent.
