The Bureau of Labor Statistics \href{https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ximpim.nr0.htm}{reports} changes in the prices of imports and exports. Over the year ending January 2026, \textbf{US import prices} were unchanged (see {\color{paleblue}\textbf{---}}), following virtually no change in December 2025 and a decrease of 0.1 percent in November. Excluding fuels, US import prices increased 1.2 percent in January 2026 and grew 0.8 percent in December 2025. In 2019, US import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.3 percent. Excluding fuels, import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.1 percent in 2019.

\textbf{Prices of US exports} grew 2.6 percent over the year ending January 2026 (see {\color{blue!90!black}\textbf{---}}), following increases of 3.4 percent in December 2025, and 3.3 percent in November. In 2019, export prices decreased at an average rate of 0.8 percent.
