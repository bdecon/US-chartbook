The Bureau of Labor Statistics \href{https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ximpim.nr0.htm}{report} changes in the prices of imports and exports. Over the year ending November 2025, \textbf{US import prices} were unchanged (see {\color{cyan!58!white}\textbf{---}}), following virtually no change in September and a decrease of 0.3 percent in August. Excluding fuels, US import prices increased 0.7 percent in November 2025 and grew 0.4 percent in September. In 2019, US import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.3 percent. Excluding fuels, import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.1 percent in 2019.

\textbf{Prices of US exports} grew 3.3 percent over the year ending November 2025 (see {\color{blue!90!black}\textbf{---}}), following increases of 3.9 percent in September, and 3.2 percent in August. In 2019, export prices decreased at an average rate of 0.8 percent.
