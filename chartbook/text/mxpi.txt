The Bureau of Labor Statistics \href{https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ximpim.nr0.htm}{reports} changes in the prices of imports and exports. Over the year ending March 2026, \textbf{US import prices} grew 2.1 percent (see {\color{paleblue}\textbf{---}}), following increases of one percent in February and 0.3 percent in January. Excluding fuels, US import prices increased 2.8 percent in March 2026 and grew 2.2 percent in February. In 2019, US import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.3 percent. Excluding fuels, import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.1 percent in 2019.

\textbf{Prices of US exports} grew 5.6 percent over the year ending March 2026 (see {\color{blue!90!black}\textbf{---}}), following increases of 3.8 percent in February and 2.5 percent in January. In 2019, export prices decreased at an average rate of 0.8 percent.
