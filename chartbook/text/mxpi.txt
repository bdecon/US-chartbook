The Bureau of Labor Statistics \href{https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ximpim.nr0.htm}{report} changes in the prices of imports and exports. Over the year ending September 2025, \textbf{US import prices} grew 0.3 percent (see {\color{cyan!58!white}\textbf{---}}), following virtually no change in August and 0.4 percent in July. Excluding fuels, US import prices increased 0.8 percent in September 2025 and grew 0.8 percent in August. In 2019, US import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.3 percent. Excluding fuels, import prices decreased at an average rate of 1.1 percent in 2019.

\textbf{Prices of US exports} grew 3.8 percent over the year ending September 2025 (see {\color{blue!90!black}\textbf{---}}), following increases of 3.2 percent in August, and 2.4 percent in July. In 2019, export prices decreased at an average rate of 0.8 percent.
