The number of Social Security beneficiaries has increased from 53.4 million in 2010, or 17.2 percent of the population, to 70.4 million in November 2025, equivalent to 20.5 percent of the population. 

Medicare enrollment has grown from 15.3 percent of the population in 2010 to 20.2 percent. Total Medicare enrollment is 69.3 million in August 2025. Medicaid, which was expanded in 2014, and the Children's Health Insurance Program (CHIP), increased enrollment from 17.4 percent of the population in 2010 to 22.7 percent in July 2025. The total enrollment for Medicaid and CHIP is 77.6 million people.
