Much of the increase in GDP over the past 30 years comes from consumer spending. Consumer spending (see {\color{yellow!45!orange}\textbf{---}}) is equivalent to \$64,479 per person in 2026 Q2, a price-adjusted increase of \$31,510 since 1989.

Gross private domestic investment (see {\color{navyblue}\textbf{---}}) is equivalent to \$16,683 per person in 2026 Q2. Government spending and investment (see {\color{paleblue}\textbf{---}}) totals \$16,175 per person. The trade deficit, equivalent to \$2,555 per person, is subtracted, to capture only domestic production (see {\color{forestgreen}\textbf{---}}).
