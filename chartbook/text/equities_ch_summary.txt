The \href{https://us.spindices.com/indices/equity/sp-500}{S\&P 500} (see {\color{green!80!blue!90!black}\textbf{---}}) is a market-cap-weighted stock market index based on 500 large companies listed on US exchanges. As of September 8, 2025, the S\&P 500 has increased at an annual rate of nine percent since 1989, and 15.3 percent since 2019. The \href{https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/index/comp}{Nasdaq} composite index (see {\color{blue!60!cyan}\textbf{---}}) includes nearly all companies listed on the Nasdaq stock exchange. The Nasdaq index increased at an annual rate of 11.7 percent since 1989, and 19.4 percent since 2019.

The \href{https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/\%5EDJI/}{Dow 30} industrial average (see {\color{red!60!purple}\textbf{---}}) is an index based on 30 large and prominent companies listed on US exchanges. The measure is used as a proxy for the performance of the largest companies, and increased at an annual rate of 8.7 percent since 1989 and 10.5 percent since 2019.
