In August 2025, a seasonally-adjusted annual rate of 1,330,000 new residential housing units were authorized by building permits, the lowest level since May 2020 (see {\color{blue!70!green}\textbf{---}}). Permits issued decreased by 32,000 (-2.3 percent) (annualized) over the previous month, decreased by 146,000 (-9.9 percent) over last August, and decreased by 203,000 (-13.2 percent) total over the past five years.
