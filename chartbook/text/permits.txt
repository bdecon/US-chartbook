In December 2025, a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,455,000 new residential housing units were authorized by building permits, the highest level since March 2025 (see {\color{blue!70!green}\textbf{---}}). Permits issued increased by 67,000 (4.8 percent) (annualized) over the previous month, decreased by 25,000 (-1.7 percent) over last December, and decreased by 297,000 (-17.0 percent) total over the past five years.
