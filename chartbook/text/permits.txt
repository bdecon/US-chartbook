In May 2025, a seasonally-adjusted annual rate of 1,394,000 new residential housing units were authorized by building permits, the lowest level since June 2020 (see {\color{blue!70!green}\textbf{---}}). Permits issued decreased by 28,000 (-2.0 percent) (annualized) over the previous month, decreased by 13,000 (-0.9 percent) over last May, and increased by 144,000 (11.5 percent) total over the past five years.
