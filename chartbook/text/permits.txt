In April 2025, a seasonally-adjusted annual rate of 1,412,000 new residential housing units were authorized by building permits, the lowest level since May 2024 (see {\color{blue!70!green}\textbf{---}}). Permits issued decreased by 69,000 (-4.7 percent) (annualized) over the previous month, decreased by 47,000 (-3.2 percent) over last April, and increased by 336,000 (31.2 percent) total over the past five years.
