In March 2026, a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,363,000 new residential housing units were authorized by building permits, the lowest level since August 2025 (see {\color{blue!70!green}\textbf{---}}). Permits issued decreased by 175,000 (-11.4 percent) (annualized) over the previous month, decreased by 118,000 (-8.0 percent) over last March, and decreased by 393,000 (-22.4 percent) total over the past five years.
