In July 2026, a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,433,000 new residential housing units were authorized by building permits, the highest level since February 2026 (see {\color{blue!70!green}\textbf{---}}). Permits issued increased by 59,000 (4.3 percent) over the previous month, increased by 33,000 (2.4 percent) over last July, and decreased by 97,000 (-6.3 percent) total over the past three years.
