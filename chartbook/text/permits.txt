In December 2025, a seasonally-adjusted annual rate of 1,448,000 new residential housing units were authorized by building permits, the highest level since March 2025 (see {\color{blue!70!green}\textbf{---}}). Permits issued increased by 60,000 (4.3 percent) (annualized) over the previous month, decreased by 32,000 (-2.2 percent) over last December, and decreased by 304,000 (-17.4 percent) total over the past five years.
