In January 2026, a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,376,000 new residential housing units were authorized by building permits, the lowest level since August 2025 (see {\color{blue!70!green}\textbf{---}}). Permits issued decreased by 79,000 (-5.4 percent) (annualized) over the previous month, decreased by 84,000 (-5.8 percent) over last January, and decreased by 479,000 (-25.8 percent) total over the past five years.
