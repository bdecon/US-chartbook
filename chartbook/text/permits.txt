In July 2025, a seasonally-adjusted annual rate of 1,362,000 new residential housing units were authorized by building permits, the lowest level since June 2020 (see {\color{blue!70!green}\textbf{---}}). Permits issued decreased by 31,000 (-2.2 percent) (annualized) over the previous month, decreased by 74,000 (-5.2 percent) over last July, and decreased by 184,000 (-11.9 percent) total over the past five years.
