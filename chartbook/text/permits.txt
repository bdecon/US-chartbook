In January 2026, a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,386,000 new residential housing units were authorized by building permits, the lowest level since August 2025 (see {\color{blue!70!green}\textbf{---}}). Permits issued decreased by 69,000 (-4.7 percent) (annualized) over the previous month, decreased by 74,000 (-5.1 percent) over last January, and decreased by 469,000 (-25.3 percent) total over the past five years.
