For those age 16 to 64 with disabilities, the employment rate is 38.5 percent in August 2025 (see {\color{blue}\textbf{---}}), a one-year increase of 2.1 percentage points, and a 6.9 percentage point increase since 2021.

In 2013, during the sluggish recovery from the great recession, the employment rate for those age 16 to 64 with a disability averaged 26.8 percent.
