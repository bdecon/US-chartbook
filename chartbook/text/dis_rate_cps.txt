For those age 16 to 64 with disabilities, the employment rate is 37.3 percent in April 2025 (see {\color{blue}\textbf{---}}), a one-year decrease of 0.5 percentage point, and a 7.1 percentage point increase since 2021.

In 2013, during the sluggish recovery from the great recession, the employment rate for those age 16 to 64 with a disability averaged 26.8 percent.
