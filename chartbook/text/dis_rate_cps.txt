For those age 16 to 64 with disabilities, the employment rate is 38.1 percent in September 2025 (see {\color{blue}\textbf{---}}), a one-year increase of 0.6 percentage point, and a 5.3 percentage point increase since 2021.

In 2013, during the sluggish recovery from the great recession, the employment rate for those age 16 to 64 with a disability averaged 26.8 percent.
