For those age 16 to 64 with disabilities, the employment rate is 38.5 percent in March 2026 (see {\color{blue}\textbf{---}}), a one-year increase of 1.4 percentage points, and a 7.6 percentage point increase since 2019.

In 2013, during the sluggish recovery from the Great Recession, the employment rate for those age 16 to 64 with a disability averaged 26.8 percent.
