In June 2025, the age 16 and older employment rate is below 60 percent in 24 states. One year prior, in June 2024, the employment rate was below 60 percent in 20 states. The rate is above 65 percent in seven states, in the latest month, and in eight states in June 2024.

The states with the highest employment rates in June 2025 are North Dakota (68.7\%), South Dakota (68.3\%), and the District of Columbia (68.1\%). The states with the lowest employment rates are West Virginia (52.9\%), Mississippi (53.3\%), and New Mexico (55.5\%).
