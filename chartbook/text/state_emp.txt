In July 2025, the age 16 and older employment rate is below 60 percent in 22 states. One year prior, in July 2024, the employment rate was below 60 percent in 20 states. The rate is above 65 percent in seven states, in the latest month, and in nine states in July 2024.

The states with the highest employment rates in July 2025 are North Dakota (68.5 percent), the District of Columbia (68.2 percent), and South Dakota (68.0 percent). The states with the lowest employment rates are West Virginia (52.8 percent), Mississippi (53.5 percent), and New Mexico (55.0 percent).
