In November 2025, the age 16 and older employment rate is below 60 percent in 23 states. One year prior, in November 2024, the employment rate was below 60 percent in 22 states. The rate is above 65 percent in eight states, in the latest month, and in six states in November 2024.

The states with the highest employment rates in November 2025 are Nebraska (68.0 percent), North Dakota (67.4 percent), and South Dakota (66.5 percent). The states with the lowest employment rates are West Virginia (51.9 percent), Mississippi (53.3 percent), and Florida (54.8 percent).
