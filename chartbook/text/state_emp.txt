In July 2026, the age 16 and older employment rate is below 60 percent in 27 states. One year prior, in July 2025, the employment rate was below 60 percent in 23 states. The rate is above 65 percent in four states, in the latest month, and in eight states in July 2025.

The states with the highest employment rates in July 2026 are North Dakota (68.2 percent), Nebraska (67.5 percent), and the District of Columbia (66.8 percent). The states with the lowest employment rates are West Virginia (52.2 percent), Mississippi (52.8 percent), and New Mexico (53.7 percent).
