In March 2025, the age 16 and older employment rate is below 60 percent in 26 states. One year prior, in March 2024, the employment rate was below 60 percent in 20 states. The rate is above 65 percent in six states, in the latest month, and in eight states in March 2024.

The states with the highest employment rates in March 2025 are the District of Columbia (68.6\%), Nebraska (67.5\%), and South Dakota (67.4\%). The states with the lowest employment rates are West Virginia (51.5\%), Mississippi (54.1\%), and New Mexico (55.2\%).
