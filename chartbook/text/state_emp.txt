In September 2025, the age 16 and older employment rate is below 60 percent in 25 states. One year prior, in September 2024, the employment rate was below 60 percent in 22 states. The rate is above 65 percent in eight states, in the latest month, and in eight states in September 2024.

The states with the highest employment rates in September 2025 are North Dakota (68.2 percent), Nebraska (67.7 percent), and South Dakota (67.2 percent). The states with the lowest employment rates are West Virginia (52.5 percent), Mississippi (53.7 percent), and New Mexico (55.1 percent).
