In August 2025, the age 16 and older employment rate is below 60 percent in 24 states. One year prior, in August 2024, the employment rate was below 60 percent in 21 states. The rate is above 65 percent in five states, in the latest month, and in seven states in August 2024.

The states with the highest employment rates in August 2025 are North Dakota (68.4 percent), Nebraska (67.9 percent), and South Dakota (67.3 percent). The states with the lowest employment rates are West Virginia (52.4 percent), Mississippi (53.0 percent), and New Mexico (54.9 percent).
