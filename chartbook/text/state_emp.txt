In May 2025, the age 16 and older employment rate is below 60 percent in 25 states. One year prior, in May 2024, the employment rate was below 60 percent in 21 states. The rate is above 65 percent in six states, in the latest month, and in seven states in May 2024.

The states with the highest employment rates in May 2025 are North Dakota (68.2\%), South Dakota (67.8\%), and Nebraska (67.7\%). The states with the lowest employment rates are West Virginia (52.3\%), Mississippi (53.7\%), and New Mexico (55.2\%).
