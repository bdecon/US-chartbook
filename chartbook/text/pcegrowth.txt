Consumer spending is also \href{https://www.bea.gov/data/consumer-spending/main}{reported} on a monthly basis. In August 2025, consumer spending totals \$61,631 per capita, on an annualized basis. Inflation- and population-adjusted consumer spending increased 2.2 percent over the year ending August 2025 (see {\color{green!70!olive!60!black}\textbf{---}}), in line with the previous year rate (an increase of 2.1 percent over the year ending August 2024).
