The consumer sentiment index combines views on current and future economic conditions. In January 2026, the index tracking views on current economic conditions was 55.4, compared to 50.4 in December 2025, and 110.8 in 2019 (see {\color{blue!50!cyan}\textbf{---}}).

In January 2026, the index tracking consumer expectations for future economic conditions was 57.0, compared to 54.6 in December 2025, and 86.5 in 2019 (see {\color{green!60!black}\textbf{---}}).
