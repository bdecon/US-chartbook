The consumer sentiment index combines views on current and future economic conditions. In September 2026, the index tracking views on current economic conditions was 50.9, compared to 51.9 in August 2026, and 110.8 in 2019 (see {\color{skyblue}\textbf{---}}).

In September 2026, the index tracking consumer expectations for future economic conditions was 46.3, compared to 51.5 in August 2026, and 86.5 in 2019 (see {\color{forestgreen}\textbf{---}}).
