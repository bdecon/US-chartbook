The consumer sentiment index combines views on current and future economic conditions. In June 2025, the index tracking views on current economic conditions was 64.8, compared to 58.9 in May 2025, and 110.8 in 2019 (see {\color{blue!50!cyan}\textbf{---}}).

In June 2025, the index tracking consumer expectations for future economic conditions was 58.1, compared to 47.9 in May 2025, and 86.5 in 2019 (see {\color{green!60!black}\textbf{---}}).
