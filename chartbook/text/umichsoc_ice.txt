The consumer sentiment index combines views on current and future economic conditions. In April 2026, the index tracking views on current economic conditions was 52.5, compared to 55.8 in March 2026, and 110.8 in 2019 (see {\color{skyblue}\textbf{---}}).

In April 2026, the index tracking consumer expectations for future economic conditions was 48.1, compared to 51.7 in March 2026, and 86.5 in 2019 (see {\color{forestgreen}\textbf{---}}).
