The consumer sentiment index combines views on current and future economic conditions. In December 2025, the index tracking views on current economic conditions was 50.4, compared to 51.1 in November 2025, and 110.8 in 2019 (see {\color{blue!50!cyan}\textbf{---}}).

In December 2025, the index tracking consumer expectations for future economic conditions was 54.6, compared to 51.0 in November 2025, and 86.5 in 2019 (see {\color{green!60!black}\textbf{---}}).
