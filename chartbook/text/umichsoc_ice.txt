The consumer sentiment index combines views on current and future economic conditions. In May 2026, the index tracking views on current economic conditions was 45.8, compared to 52.5 in April 2026, and 110.8 in 2019 (see {\color{skyblue}\textbf{---}}).

In May 2026, the index tracking consumer expectations for future economic conditions was 44.1, compared to 48.1 in April 2026, and 86.5 in 2019 (see {\color{forestgreen}\textbf{---}}).
