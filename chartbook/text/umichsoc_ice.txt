The consumer sentiment index combines views on current and future economic conditions. In September 2025, the index tracking views on current economic conditions was 60.4, compared to 61.7 in August 2025, and 110.8 in 2019 (see {\color{blue!50!cyan}\textbf{---}}).

In September 2025, the index tracking consumer expectations for future economic conditions was 51.7, compared to 55.9 in August 2025, and 86.5 in 2019 (see {\color{green!60!black}\textbf{---}}).
