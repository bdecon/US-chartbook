The consumer sentiment index combines views on current and future economic conditions. In April 2025, the index tracking views on current economic conditions was 59.8, compared to 63.8 in March 2025, and 110.8 in 2019 (see {\color{blue!50!cyan}\textbf{---}}).

In April 2025, the index tracking consumer expectations for future economic conditions was 47.3, compared to 52.6 in March 2025, and 86.5 in 2019 (see {\color{green!60!black}\textbf{---}}).
