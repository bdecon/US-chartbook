The consumer sentiment index combines views on current and future economic conditions. In August 2026, the index tracking views on current economic conditions was 51.9, compared to 54.8 in July 2026, and 110.8 in 2019 (see {\color{skyblue}\textbf{---}}).

In August 2026, the index tracking consumer expectations for future economic conditions was 51.5, compared to 55.4 in July 2026, and 86.5 in 2019 (see {\color{forestgreen}\textbf{---}}).
