The consumer sentiment index combines views on current and future economic conditions. In March 2026, the index tracking views on current economic conditions was 55.8, compared to 56.6 in February 2026, and 110.8 in 2019 (see {\color{skyblue}\textbf{---}}).

In March 2026, the index tracking consumer expectations for future economic conditions was 51.7, compared to 56.6 in February 2026, and 86.5 in 2019 (see {\color{forestgreen}\textbf{---}}).
