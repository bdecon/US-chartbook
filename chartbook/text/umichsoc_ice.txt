The consumer sentiment index combines views on current and future economic conditions. In August 2025, the index tracking views on current economic conditions was 61.7, compared to 68.0 in July 2025, and 110.8 in 2019 (see {\color{blue!50!cyan}\textbf{---}}).

In August 2025, the index tracking consumer expectations for future economic conditions was 55.9, compared to 57.7 in July 2025, and 86.5 in 2019 (see {\color{green!60!black}\textbf{---}}).
