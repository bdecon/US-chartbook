The consumer sentiment index combines views on current and future economic conditions. In November 2025, the index tracking views on current economic conditions was 51.1, compared to 58.6 in October 2025, and 110.8 in 2019 (see {\color{blue!50!cyan}\textbf{---}}).

In November 2025, the index tracking consumer expectations for future economic conditions was 51.0, compared to 50.3 in October 2025, and 86.5 in 2019 (see {\color{green!60!black}\textbf{---}}).
