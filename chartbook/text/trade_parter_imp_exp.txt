Imports of goods have increased from 8.4 percent of GDP in 1989 to 10.9 percent of GDP in the year ending January 2026. Goods imports from China increased by 2.5 percent of GDP from 1989 to 2015, and have since fallen by 1.8 percentage points to 0.9 percent of GDP. Goods imports from Mexico have increased by 1.3 percent of GDP since 1989. Goods imports from Japan have fallen by 1.2 percent of GDP.

 Exports of goods have increased by 0.7 percent of GDP since 1989. The largest buyers of US-made goods are Mexico, Canada, and China. Exports to these three countries make up 35.3 percent of exports and are equivalent to 2.5 percent of GDP over the year ending January 2026. Exports to the European Union currently total 1.3 percent of GDP.
