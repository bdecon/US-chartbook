On August 4, 2025, the \href{https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist/RWTCD.htm}{spot price} for a barrel of west Texas intermediate (WTI) \textbf{crude oil} is \$67.33 (see {\color{red!80!purple}\textbf{---}}). Over the past year, this measure of oil prices decreased 12.2 percent. Over the past five years, the price increased 59.0 percent. The WTI price is currently \$67 below its peak monthly average price of \$134 per barrel in June 2008.
