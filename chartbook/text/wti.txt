On May 4, 2026, the \href{https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist/RWTCD.htm}{current market price} for a barrel of west Texas intermediate (WTI) \textbf{crude oil} is \$109.76 (see {\color{red!80!purple!65!white}\textbf{---}}), the highest level since June 2022. This price increased 76.6 percent over the past year, and increased 68.4 percent over the past five years. The WTI price is currently \$24 below its peak monthly average price of \$134 per barrel in June 2008.
