On March 23, 2026, the \href{https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist/RWTCD.htm}{current market price} for a barrel of west Texas intermediate (WTI) \textbf{crude oil} is \$89.33 (see {\color{red!80!purple!65!white}\textbf{---}}), the highest level since September 2023. This price increased 30.9 percent over the past year, and increased 43.3 percent over the past five years. The WTI price is currently \$45 below its peak monthly average price of \$134 per barrel in June 2008.
