The Office of Management and Budget \href{https://www.whitehouse.gov/omb/historical-tables/}{report} \textbf{federal interest outlays} of \$970 billion in fiscal year 2025 (the year beginning October 1, 2024), compared to \$881 billion in fiscal year 2024.

Put into the context of the size of the economy, federal interest outlays in fiscal year 2024 were equivalent to 3.04 percent of GDP (see {\color{magenta}\textbf{---}}), following 2.41 percent of GDP in FY2023 and 1.86 percent in FY2022, and compared to an average of 2.9 percent in the 1990s, when interest rates were substantially higher.
