Looking more closely at recent industrial production growth, the latest one-year growth rate, covering June 2025, is slightly above the five-year average, and in line with the May growth rate. 

By market group, the latest low growth is largely the result of an increase in non-energy materials, and partially offset by a decrease in other categories. By industry group, the latest low growth is also largely the result of an increase in durable manufacturing, and partially offset by a decrease in other categories.
