Looking more closely at recent industrial production growth, the latest one-year growth rate, covering July 2026, is in line with the five-year average, and in line with the June growth rate. 

By market group, the latest growth is driven largely by an increase in business equipment and non-industrial supplies, and partially offset by a decrease in consumer goods. By industry group, the latest growth is also driven largely by an increase in durable manufacturing, and partially offset by a decrease in nondurable manufacturing.
