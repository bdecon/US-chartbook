Looking more closely at recent industrial production growth, the latest one-year growth rate, covering August 2026, is slightly above the five-year average, and in line with the July growth rate. 

By market group, the latest growth is relatively broad-based. The main contribution is an increase in business equipment and non-industrial supplies. By industry group, the latest growth is driven largely by an increase in durable manufacturing, and supported by an increase in electric and gas utilities, and partially offset by a decrease in nondurable manufacturing.
