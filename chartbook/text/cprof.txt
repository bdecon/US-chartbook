In the second quarter of 2026, corporate profits were \$4.71 trillion, equivalent to 19.3 percent of the income paid to US nationals after depreciation costs (net national income). Of this, \$2.20 trillion, equivalent to nine percent of net national income, was paid out as dividends \seebox{blue!70!purple}, \$1,679 billion was retained (corporate saving, see \cbox{cyan!50!white}), and \$832 billion, 17.7 percent of corporate profits, went to corporate income tax \seebox{red!80!orange}.

In 2019, corporate profits were 15.1 percent of net national income. Dividends were equivalent to 8.6 percent, corporate saving was 4.6 percent, and corporate income taxes were 1.8 percent of net national income and 12 percent of corporate profits.
