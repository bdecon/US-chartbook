Hourly wages for production and non-supervisory workers in private goods-producing sectors average \$32.43 in July 2025 (see {\color{cyan!80!green!92!blue}\textbf{---}}). In July 2019, the average hourly wage for the sector is \$31.14, after adjusting for inflation.

Private service-providing industry wages average \$31.12 for production and non-supervisory workers in July 2025. The inflation-adjusted equivalent is \$29.32 in July 2019 (see {\color{blue!70!violet}\textbf{---}}).
