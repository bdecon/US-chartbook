Hourly wages for production and non-supervisory workers in private goods-producing sectors average \$32.23 in April 2025 (see {\color{cyan!80!green!92!blue}\textbf{---}}). In April 2019, the average hourly wage for the sector is \$30.85, after adjusting for inflation.

Private service-providing industry wages average \$30.83 for production and non-supervisory workers in April 2025. The inflation-adjusted equivalent is \$28.95 in April 2019 (see {\color{blue!70!violet}\textbf{---}}).
