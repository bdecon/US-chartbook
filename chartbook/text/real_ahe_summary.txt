Hourly wages for production and non-supervisory workers in private goods-producing sectors average \$33.73 in April 2026 (see {\color{cyan!80!green!92!blue}\textbf{---}}). In April 2019, the average hourly wage for the sector was \$32.01, after adjusting for inflation.

Private service-providing industry wages average \$31.93 for production and non-supervisory workers in April 2026. The inflation-adjusted equivalent was \$30.05 in April 2019 (see {\color{blue!70!violet}\textbf{---}}).
