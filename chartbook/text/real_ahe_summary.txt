Hourly wages for production and non-supervisory workers in private goods-producing sectors average \$32.74 in September 2025 (see {\color{cyan!80!green!92!blue}\textbf{---}}). In September 2019, the average hourly wage for the sector is \$31.46, after adjusting for inflation.

Private service-providing industry wages average \$31.29 for production and non-supervisory workers in September 2025. The inflation-adjusted equivalent is \$29.64 in September 2019 (see {\color{blue!70!violet}\textbf{---}}).
