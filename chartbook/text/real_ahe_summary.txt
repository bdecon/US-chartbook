Hourly wages for production and non-supervisory workers in private goods-producing sectors average \$33.97 in September 2026 (see {\color{cyan!80!green!92!blue}\textbf{---}}). In September 2019, the average hourly wage for the sector was \$32.62, after adjusting for inflation.

Private service-providing industry wages average \$32.33 for production and non-supervisory workers in September 2026. The inflation-adjusted equivalent was \$30.73 in September 2019 (see {\color{blue!70!violet}\textbf{---}}).
