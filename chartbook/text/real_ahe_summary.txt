Hourly wages for production and non-supervisory workers in private goods-producing sectors average \$33.42 in February 2026 (see {\color{cyan!80!green!92!blue}\textbf{---}}). In February 2019, the average hourly wage for the sector was \$31.53, after adjusting for inflation.

Private service-providing industry wages average \$31.74 for production and non-supervisory workers in February 2026. The inflation-adjusted equivalent was \$29.65 in February 2019 (see {\color{blue!70!violet}\textbf{---}}).
