Historical stock market \href{http://www.econ.yale.edu/~shiller/data.htm}{data} from Robert Shiller show the \textbf{inflation-adjusted trailing twenty-year annual return} of the S\&P 500 is 8.5 percent as of June 2026 (see {\color{tealgreen}\textbf{---}}). Ultra-long-term real returns are currently low relative to the average trailing twenty-year real annual return of 10.1 percent during 1995--2005. The trailing ten-year real return was 11.6 percent, as of June 2026, and 10.7 percent during 1995--2005 (see {\color{blue}\textbf{---}}).
