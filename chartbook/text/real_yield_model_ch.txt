Over the three months ending March 2026, two-year treasury yields increased 0.07 percentage point, real yields increased 0.54 percentage point, and inflation expectations fell 0.47 point. Ten-year treasury yields were unchanged, real yields grew 0.08 point, and inflation expectations fell 0.09 point.

Over the five years ending March 2026, the yield on two-year treasuries increased 3.13 percentage points, the real yield grew 1.94 points, and inflation expectations grew 1.19 points. For ten-year treasuries, the yield increased 3.27 percentage points, the real yield grew 2.28 points, and expected inflation increased 0.99 percentage point.
