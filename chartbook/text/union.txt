Over the 12 months ending February 2026, the union membership rate averaged 10.1 percent \seebox{violet}. The coverage rate, which includes nonmembers that are covered under a union contract, was 11.2 percent. During the 12-month period, an average of 131.8 million workers were not represented by a union, 14.8 million workers were union members, and an additional 1.7 million workers, or 1.1 percent of the workforce, reported no union affiliation but were covered by a union contract \seebox{magenta}. 

 One year prior, over the 12 months ending February 2025, the union membership rate was 9.9 percent, and the coverage rate was 11.2 percent. From February 2025 to February 2026, the 12-month average number of nonunion workers increased by 1.2 million, while the number of workers represented by unions increased by 246,000.
