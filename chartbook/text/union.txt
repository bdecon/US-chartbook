Over the 12 months ending March 2026, the union membership rate averaged 10.1 percent \seebox{violet}. The coverage rate, which includes nonmembers that are covered under a union contract, was 11.2 percent. During the 12-month period, an average of 16.4 million workers were covered by a union contract -- 14.7 million union members and 1.6 million nonmembers covered by contracts \seebox{magenta} -- while 130.2 million workers were not covered. 

 One year prior, over the 12 months ending March 2025, the union membership rate was 9.9 percent, and the coverage rate was 11.1 percent. From March 2025 to March 2026, the 12-month average number of nonunion workers increased by 1.1 million, while the number of workers represented by unions increased by 190,000.
