Wealth has also, typically but not always, grown faster than income. Over the past 30 years, real income grew 2.6 percent per year. In 2025 Q1, inflation-adjusted wealth increased by 1.2 percent \seebox{cyan!40!white}, and inflation-adjusted income increased by 1.6 percent (see {\color{blue!50!violet}\textbf{---}}). Over the past three years, inflation-adjusted wealth was virtually unchanged, while real income grew by 3.2 percent per year.
