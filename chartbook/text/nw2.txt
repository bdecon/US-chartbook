Wealth has also, typically but not always, grown faster than income. Over the past 30 years, real income grew 2.7 percent per year. In 2025 Q4, inflation-adjusted wealth increased by 5.6 percent \seebox{cyan!40!white}, and inflation-adjusted income increased by 1.3 percent (see {\color{blue!50!violet}\textbf{---}}). Over the past three years, inflation-adjusted wealth grew at an annual rate of 5.7 percent, while real income rose by 3.1 percent per year.
