Wealth has also, typically but not always, grown faster than income. Over the past 30 years, real income grew 2.7 percent per year. In 2025 Q2, inflation-adjusted wealth increased by 3.6 percent \seebox{cyan!40!white}, and inflation-adjusted income increased by two percent (see {\color{blue!50!violet}\textbf{---}}). Over the past three years, inflation-adjusted wealth grew at an annual rate of 3.1 percent, while real income grew by 3.6 percent per year.
