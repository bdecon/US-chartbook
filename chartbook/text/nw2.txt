Wealth has also, typically but not always, grown faster than income. Over the past 30 years, real income grew 2.6 percent per year. In 2026 Q1, inflation-adjusted wealth increased by 4.7 percent \seebox{cyan!40!white}, and inflation-adjusted income increased by 0.7 percent (see {\color{blue!50!violet}\textbf{---}}). Over the past three years, inflation-adjusted wealth grew at an annual rate of 4.6 percent, while real income rose by two percent per year.
