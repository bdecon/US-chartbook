Wealth has also, typically but not always, grown faster than income. Over the past 30 years, real income grew 2.6 percent per year. In 2026 Q2, inflation-adjusted wealth increased by 7.6 percent \seebox{cyan!40!white}, and inflation-adjusted income decreased by 0.1 percent (see {\color{blue!50!violet}\textbf{---}}). Over the past three years, inflation-adjusted wealth grew at an annual rate of 5.5 percent, while real income rose by 1.6 percent per year.
