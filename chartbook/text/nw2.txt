Wealth has also, typically but not always, grown faster than income. Over the past 30 years, real income grew 2.7 percent per year. In 2025 Q3, inflation-adjusted wealth increased by 4.8 percent \seebox{cyan!40!white}, and inflation-adjusted income increased by 1.5 percent (see {\color{blue!50!violet}\textbf{---}}). Over the past three years, inflation-adjusted wealth grew at an annual rate of 5.3 percent, while real income rose by 3.3 percent per year.
