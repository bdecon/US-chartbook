Over the year ending 2025 Q3, labor receives 67.4 percent of net domestic income (see {\color{blue!50!cyan}\textbf{---}}). Labor's share increased 0.1 percentage point over the past year. For context, one percent of net domestic income translates to \$234 billion per year, which is \$1,470 per worker. 

 Labor's share in the corporate sector is 72.1 percent in 2025 Q3 (see {\color{green!60!blue}\textbf{---}}). The corporate sector has well-defined accounting, which is useful for this analysis. The corporate labor share is currently 10.9 percentage points below its 30-year high of 83 percent in 1993 Q1.
