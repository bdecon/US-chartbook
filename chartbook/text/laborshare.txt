Over the year ending 2026 Q1, labor's share is 67.3 percent of net domestic income (see {\color{skyblue}\textbf{---}}). Labor's share decreased 0.1 percentage point over the past year. For context, one percent of net domestic income translates to \$240 billion per year, which is \$1,520 per worker. 

 Labor's share in the corporate sector is 71.8 percent in 2026 Q1 (see {\color{green!60!blue}\textbf{---}}). The corporate sector has well-defined accounting, which is useful for this analysis. The corporate labor share is currently 11.2 percentage points below its 30-year high of 83 percent in 1993 Q1.
