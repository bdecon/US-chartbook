Over the year ending 2025 Q1, labor receives 67.4 percent of net domestic income (see {\color{blue!50!cyan}\textbf{---}}). Labor's share decreased 0.1 percentage point over the past year. For context, one percent of net domestic income translates to \$230 billion per year, which is \$1,440 per worker. 

 Labor's share in the corporate sector is 71.7 percent in 2025 Q1 (see {\color{green!60!blue}\textbf{---}}). The corporate sector has well-defined accounting, which is useful for this analysis. The corporate labor share is currently 11.3 percentage points below its 30-year high of 83.0 percent in 1993 Q1.
