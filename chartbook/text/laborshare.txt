Over the year ending 2026 Q2, labor's share is 65.7 percent of net domestic income (see {\color{skyblue}\textbf{---}}). Labor's share decreased 0.8 percentage point over the past year. For context, one percent of net domestic income translates to \$252 billion per year, which is \$1,590 per worker. 

 Labor's share in the corporate sector is 68.7 percent in 2026 Q2 (see {\color{green!60!blue}\textbf{---}}). The corporate sector has well-defined accounting, which is useful for this analysis. The corporate labor share is currently 14.2 percentage points below its 30-year high of 83 percent in 1993 Q1.
