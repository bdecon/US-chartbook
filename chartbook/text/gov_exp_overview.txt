As of 2025 Q2, government current expenditures total \$10.4 trillion, which is 34.4 percent of GDP (see {\color{black!80!white}\textbf{---}}). One year prior, in 2024 Q2, government current expenditures total 33.8 percent of GDP. Government spending peaked during the COVID-19 pandemic, averaging 41.2 percent of GDP from 2020 to 2021. Since 1989, government spending averages 33.4 percent of GDP.
