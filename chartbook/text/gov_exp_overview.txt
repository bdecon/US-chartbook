As of 2026 Q1, government current expenditures total \$11.0 trillion, which is 34.4 percent of GDP (see {\color{darkgray}\textbf{---}}). One year prior, in 2025 Q1, government current expenditures totaled 34.7 percent of GDP. Government spending peaked during the COVID-19 pandemic, averaging 41.2 percent of GDP from 2020 to 2021. Since 1989, government spending has averaged 33.4 percent of GDP.
