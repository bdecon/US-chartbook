Of those newly employed in December 2025, 72.8 percent were out of the labor force the month prior (see {\color{green!60!teal!80!black}\textbf{---}}), compared to 70.4 percent in December 2024; the rest were previously unemployed.

When unemployment is low, the newly employed are more likely to come from outside of the labor force. In April 2023, when the unemployment rate was 3.4 percent, 74.6 percent of the newly employed had not looked for work the previous month.
