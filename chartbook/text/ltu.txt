This measure of \textbf{long-term unemployment} peaked at 4.54 percent of the labor force in April 2010, but had fallen to 0.67 percent in December 2019.

 In September 2026, three million people, equivalent to 1.78 percent of the labor force, have been unemployed for at least 15 weeks (see {\color{violet!90!black}\textbf{---}}), following 1.78 percent in August and 1.75 percent in July. One year prior, in September 2025, 1.75 percent were unemployed for 15 weeks or more.
