As of August 2026, BLS \href{https://www.bls.gov/webapps/legacy/cpsatab12.htm}{reports} that 1.15 percent of the labor force have been unemployed for 27 weeks or longer, compared to 1.13 percent in August 2025 (see {\color{blue}\textbf{---}}). This measure of \textbf{long-term unemployment} peaked at 4.54 percent of the labor force in April 2010, but had fallen to 0.67 percent in December 2019.

 In August 2026, three million people, equivalent to 1.75 percent of the labor force, have been unemployed for at least 15 weeks (see {\color{violet!90!black}\textbf{---}}), following 1.75 percent in July and 1.67 percent in June. One year prior, in August 2025, 1.68 percent were unemployed for 15 weeks or more.
