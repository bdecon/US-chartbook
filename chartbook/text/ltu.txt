As of December 2025, BLS \href{https://www.bls.gov/webapps/legacy/cpsatab12.htm}{reports} that 1.08 percent of the labor force have been unemployed for 27 weeks or longer, compared to 0.87 percent in December 2024 (see {\color{blue}\textbf{---}}). This measure of \textbf{long-term unemployment} peaked at 4.54 percent of the labor force in April 2010, but had fallen to 0.67 percent in December 2019.

 In December 2025, three million people, equivalent to 1.74 percent of the labor force, have been unemployed for at least 15 weeks (see {\color{violet!90!black}\textbf{---}}), following 1.74 percent in both November and October. One year prior, in December 2024, 1.54 percent are unemployed for 15 weeks or more.
