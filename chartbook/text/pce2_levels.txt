Within consumer spending on services, housing and utilities spending totals \$11,389 on an annualized and per person basis in 2026 Q1 (see {\color{green!60!blue}\textbf{---}}) and \$10,496 in 2019 Q4.

Construction or improvement of housing is considered residential fixed investment, not consumer spending, but can be combined with spending to analyze patterns in shelter costs. In 2026 Q1, residential investment totals \$3,420 per person (see {\color{darkblue}\textbf{---}}), compared to \$3,664 in the pre-COVID data covering 2019 Q4.
