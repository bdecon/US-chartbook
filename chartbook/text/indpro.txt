The Federal Reserve industrial production index \href{https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/Current/default.htm}{measures} the real output of the industrial sector, which includes manufacturing, mining, and electric and gas utilities.

\textbf{Industrial production} increased 1.5 percent over the year ending April 2025, following an increase of 1.3 percent in March. The manufacturing-only index increased 1.2 percent over the year ending April, and contributed 0.9 percentage point to the growth of the total index. Mining contributed 0.1 percentage point, and utilities contributed 0.5 percentage point.

By market group, finished consumer goods did not contribute to one-year industrial production growth in April. Business equipment contributed 0.3 percentage point, nonindustrial supplies contributed 0.4 percentage point, and materials contributed 0.9 percentage point.
