\textbf{Industrial production} increased 1.6 percent over the year ending September 2025, following an increase of 0.9 percent in August. The manufacturing-only index grew 1.5 percent over the year ending September, and contributed 1.1 percentage points to the growth of the total index. Mining added 0.3 percentage point, and utilities contributed 0.2 point.

By market group, finished consumer goods subtracted 0.3 percentage point from growth in September. Business equipment contributed 0.9 percentage point, nonindustrial supplies contributed 0.1 point, and materials added one point.
