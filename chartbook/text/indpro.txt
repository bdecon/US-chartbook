\textbf{Industrial production} increased 0.7 percent over the year ending March 2026, following an increase of 1.2 percent in February. The manufacturing-only index grew 0.5 percent over the year ending March, and contributed 0.4 percentage point to the growth of the total index. Mining did not add, and utilities contributed 0.4 point.

By market group, finished consumer goods subtracted 0.4 percentage point from growth in March. Business equipment contributed 0.5 percentage point, nonindustrial supplies did not contribute, and materials added 0.6 point.
