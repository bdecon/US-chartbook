\textbf{Industrial production} increased 0.9 percent over the year ending August 2025, following an increase of 1.3 percent in July. The manufacturing-only index grew 0.9 percent over the year ending August, and contributed 0.7 percentage point to the growth of the total index. Mining added 0.2 percentage point, and utilities did not contribute.

By market group, finished consumer goods did not add to growth in August. Business equipment contributed 0.3 percentage point, nonindustrial supplies did not contribute, and materials added 0.6 point.
