\textbf{Industrial production} increased 1.7 percent over the year ending May 2026, following an increase of 1.4 percent in April. The manufacturing-only index grew 1.4 percent over the year ending May, and contributed 1.1 percentage points to the growth of the total index. Mining added 0.2 percentage point, and utilities contributed 0.4 point.

By market group, finished consumer goods subtracted 0.2 percentage point from growth in May. Business equipment contributed 0.7 percentage point, nonindustrial supplies contributed 0.4 point, and materials added 0.8 point.
