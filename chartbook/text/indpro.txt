The Federal Reserve industrial production index \href{https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/Current/default.htm}{measures} the real output of the industrial sector, which includes manufacturing, mining, and electric and gas utilities.

\textbf{Industrial production} increased 1.4 percent over the year ending July 2025, following an increase of 0.8 percent in June. The manufacturing-only index increased 1.4 percent over the year ending July, and contributed 1.1 percentage points to the growth of the total index. Mining contributed 0.3 percentage point, and utilities did not contribute.

By market group, finished consumer goods contributed 0.2 percentage point to one-year industrial production growth in July. Business equipment contributed 0.3 percentage point, nonindustrial supplies contributed 0.1 percentage point, and materials contributed 0.8 percentage point.
