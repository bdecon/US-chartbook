\textbf{Industrial production} increased 1.4 percent over the year ending February 2026, following an increase of 2.3 percent in January. The manufacturing-only index grew 1.3 percent over the year ending February, and contributed 0.9 percentage point to the growth of the total index. Mining added 0.2 percentage point, and utilities contributed 0.3 point.

By market group, finished consumer goods did not add to growth in February. Business equipment contributed 0.6 percentage point, nonindustrial supplies contributed 0.1 point, and materials added 0.7 point.
