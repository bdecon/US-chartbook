\textbf{Industrial production} increased 1.4 percent over the year ending July 2025, following an increase of 0.8 percent in June. The manufacturing-only index grew 1.4 percent over the year ending July, and contributed 1.1 percentage points to the growth of the total index. Mining added 0.3 percentage point, and utilities did not contribute.

By market group, finished consumer goods added 0.2 percentage point to growth in July. Business equipment contributed 0.3 percentage point, nonindustrial supplies contributed 0.1 point, and materials added 0.8 point.
