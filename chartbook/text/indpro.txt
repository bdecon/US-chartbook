\textbf{Industrial production} increased 1.4 percent over the year ending August 2026, following an increase of 1.1 percent in July. The manufacturing-only index grew 0.9 percent over the year ending August, and contributed 0.6 percentage point to the growth of the total index. Mining did not add, and utilities contributed 0.8 point.

By market group, finished consumer goods subtracted 0.3 percentage point from growth in August. Business equipment contributed 0.9 percentage point, nonindustrial supplies contributed 0.3 point, and materials added 0.7 point.
