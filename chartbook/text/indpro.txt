\textbf{Industrial production} increased 2.3 percent over the year ending January 2026, following an increase of 1.3 percent in December 2025. The manufacturing-only index grew 2.4 percent over the year ending January, and contributed 1.8 percentage points to the growth of the total index. Mining added 0.3 percentage point, and utilities contributed 0.1 point.

By market group, finished consumer goods added 0.4 percentage point to growth in January. Business equipment contributed 0.9 percentage point, nonindustrial supplies contributed 0.2 point, and materials added 0.8 point.
