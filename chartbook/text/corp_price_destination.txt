From 1989 to 2019, more than half of corporate price increases went toward unit labor costs---only 18.1 percent went to corporate profits. Non-labor input costs claim a little less than a third of the higher prices.

In the recent period, more of the increase goes to corporate profits. Since 2020, 42.8 percent of corporate price increases become corporate profits. Labor gets 41.5 percent of the increase. Non-labor input costs claim 15.7 percent of the total.
