In 2025 Q3, the US private sector is a net lender (running a surplus) of the equivalent of 4.3 percent of GDP, in line with the 4.6 percent surplus in 2019. The rest of the world is a net lender to the US to the equivalent of 3.2 percent of GDP in 2025 Q3, compared to 2.1 percent in 2019. Balancing these transactions, the government (federal, state, and local combined) is a net borrower (running a deficit) of the equivalent of 7.4 percent of GDP in 2025 Q3, compared to 6.7 percent in 2019.
