In March 2026, the M2 money stock totals \$22.8 trillion. Put into the context of overall economic activity, M2 is equivalent to 71.0 percent of GDP.

During the 1990s, the ratio of money to economic activity was falling (see {\color{magenta}\textbf{---}}). Following the Great Recession, the money supply has expanded relative to activity. Since 1989, the ratio has increased by a total of 31.3 percent.
