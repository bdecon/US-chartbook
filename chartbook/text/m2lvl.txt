In June 2025, the M2 money stock totals \$22.0 trillion. Put into the context of overall economic activity, M2 is equivalent to 72.4 percent of GDP.

During the 1990s, the ratio of money to economic activity was falling (see {\color{magenta}\textbf{---}}). Following the great recession, the money supply has expanded relative to activity. Since 1989, the ratio has increased by a total of 27.7 percent.
