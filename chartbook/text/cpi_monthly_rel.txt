Turning to one-month growth, the core CPI, which excludes food and energy, increased 0.3 percent in August 2025, or 4.2 percent annualized, substantially above the one-year core CPI inflation rate of 3.1 percent. The core CPI increased 0.3 percent in July 2025, and increased 0.2 percent in June 2025.

In August, housing prices increased 0.4 percent, (4.4 percent annualized). Over the past three months, housing prices increased at an average annualized rate of 3.6 percent, substantially above the 12-month rate of 3.1 percent. Food prices increased 0.5 percent in August, or 5.6 percent, annualized, compared to a three-month average of 3.4 percent.

Transportation prices increased at an annualized rate of 11.9 percent in August, and increased at an average annualized rate of 4.4 percent over the past three months. Energy prices increased at an annualized rate of 8.6 percent in August, and increased at an average annualized rate of 2.8 percent over the past three months.
