Turning to one-month growth, the core CPI, which excludes food and energy, increased 0.2 percent in September 2025, or 2.8 percent annualized, slightly below the one-year core CPI inflation rate of 3.0 percent. The core CPI increased 0.3 percent in both August 2025 and in July 2025.

In September, housing prices increased 0.2 percent, (1.9 percent annualized). Over the past three months, housing prices increased at an average annualized rate of three percent, in line with the 12-month rate of 3.0 percent. Food prices increased 0.2 percent in September, or three percent, annualized, compared to a three-month average of 3.1 percent.

Transportation prices increased at an annualized rate of 10.4 percent in September, and increased at an average annualized rate of 7.6 percent over the past three months. Energy prices increased at an annualized rate of 19.7 percent in September, and increased at an average annualized rate of 5.4 percent over the past three months.
