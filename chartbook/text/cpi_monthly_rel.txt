Turning to one-month growth, the core CPI, which excludes food and energy, increased 0.2 percent in April 2025, or 2.9 percent annualized, in line with the one-year core CPI inflation rate of 2.8 percent. The core CPI increased 0.1 percent in March 2025, and increased 0.2 percent in February 2025.

In April, housing prices increased 0.5 percent, (5.8 percent annualized). Over the past three months, housing prices increased at an average annualized rate of 4.7 percent, substantially above the 12-month rate of 2.8 percent. Food prices was virtually unchanged in April, or negative 0.9 percent, annualized, compared to a three-month average of 2.1 percent.

Transportation prices decreased at an annualized rate of 0.5 percent in April, and decreased at an average annualized rate of 8.2 percent over the past three months. Energy prices increased at an annualized rate of 8.3 percent in April, and decreased at an average annualized rate of 4.8 percent over the past three months.
