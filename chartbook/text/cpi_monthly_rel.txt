Turning to one-month growth, the core CPI, which excludes food and energy, increased 0.2 percent in May 2026, or 2.5 percent annualized, slightly below the one-year core CPI inflation rate of 2.9 percent. The core CPI increased 0.4 percent in April, and increased 0.2 percent in March.

In May, housing prices increased 0.2 percent (three percent annualized). Over the past three months, housing prices increased at an average annualized rate of 5.3 percent, substantially above the 12-month rate of 3.6 percent. Food prices increased 0.2 percent in May, or 1.9 percent, annualized, compared to a three-month average of 2.7 percent.

Transportation prices increased at an annualized rate of 16.9 percent in May, and increased at an average annualized rate of 33.2 percent over the past three months. Energy prices increased at an annualized rate of 58.0 percent in May, and increased at an average annualized rate of 119.9 percent over the past three months.
