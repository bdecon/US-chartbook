Turning to one-month growth, the core CPI, which excludes food and energy, increased 0.3 percent in August 2026, or 3.5 percent annualized, substantially above the one-year core CPI inflation rate of 2.4 percent. The core CPI increased 0.2 percent in July, and was virtually unchanged in June.

In August, housing prices increased 0.2 percent (2.7 percent annualized). Over the past three months, housing prices increased at an average annualized rate of 1.7 percent, substantially below the 12-month rate of 3.1 percent. Food prices increased 0.1 percent in August, or 1.5 percent, annualized, compared to a three-month average of 1.6 percent.

Transportation prices increased at an annualized rate of 15.3 percent in August, and decreased at an average annualized rate of 5.5 percent over the past three months. Energy prices increased at an annualized rate of 28.3 percent in August, and decreased at an average annualized rate of 12.9 percent over the past three months.
