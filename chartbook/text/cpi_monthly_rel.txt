Turning to one-month growth, the core CPI, which excludes food and energy, increased 0.3 percent in September 2026, or 4.1 percent annualized, substantially above the one-year core CPI inflation rate of 2.6 percent. The core CPI increased 0.3 percent in August, and increased 0.2 percent in July.

In September, housing prices increased 0.4 percent (4.4 percent annualized). Over the past three months, housing prices increased at an average annualized rate of three percent, slightly below the 12-month rate of 3.3 percent. Food prices were unchanged in September, compared to a three-month average of 1.0 percent.

Transportation prices increased at an annualized rate of 34.0 percent in September, and increased at an average annualized rate of 14.6 percent over the past three months. Energy prices increased at an annualized rate of 83.6 percent in September, and increased at an average annualized rate of 31.8 percent over the past three months.
