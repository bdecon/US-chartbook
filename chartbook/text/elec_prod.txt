Since 2011, annualized US \textbf{electricity generation} has increased 7.9 percent (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}), rising from 4,128 billion kilowatt hours in 2011 to 4,455 billion kilowatt hours over the year ending June 2025.

Over the same period, the population has increased by 10.0 percent and real GDP has increased by 39.9 percent. As a result, electricity generated per person is below the 2011 level (see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}). Moreover, the electricity required to produce a unit of real GDP fell by 22.9 percent (see {\color{cyan}\textbf{---}}).
