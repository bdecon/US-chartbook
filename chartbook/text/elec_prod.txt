Since 2011, annualized US \textbf{electricity generation} has increased ten percent (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}), rising from 4,128 terawatt hours (TWh) in 2011 to 4,541 TWh over the year ending March 2026.

Over the same period, the population has increased by 10.1 percent and real GDP has increased by 43.0 percent. As a result, electricity generated per person is near the 2011 level (see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}). Moreover, the electricity used to produce a unit of GDP fell by 22.9 percent (see {\color{cyan}\textbf{---}}).
