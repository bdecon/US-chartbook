Since 2011, annualized US \textbf{electricity generation} has increased nine percent (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}), rising from 4,128 billion kilowatt hours in 2011 to 4,500 billion kilowatt hours over the year ending November 2025.

Over the same period, the population has increased by 10.2 percent and real GDP has increased by 42.9 percent. As a result, electricity generated per person is below the 2011 level (see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}). Moreover, the electricity used to produce a unit of GDP fell by 23.6 percent (see {\color{cyan}\textbf{---}}).
