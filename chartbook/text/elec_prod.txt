Since 2011, annualized US \textbf{electricity generation} has increased 10.8 percent (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}), rising from 4,128 terawatt hours (TWh) in 2011 to 4,572 TWh over the year ending June 2026.

Over the same period, the population has increased by 10.2 percent and real GDP has increased by 43.9 percent. As a result, electricity generated per person is above the 2011 level (see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}). Moreover, the electricity used to produce a unit of GDP fell by 22.9 percent (see {\color{cyan}\textbf{---}}).
