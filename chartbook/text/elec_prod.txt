Since 2011, annualized US \textbf{electricity generation} has increased 9.5 percent (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}), rising from 4,128 terawatt hours (TWh) in 2011 to 4,520 TWh over the year ending January 2026.

Over the same period, the population has increased by 10.1 percent and real GDP has increased by 42.6 percent. As a result, electricity generated per person is near the 2011 level (see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}). Moreover, the electricity used to produce a unit of GDP fell by 23.1 percent (see {\color{cyan}\textbf{---}}).
