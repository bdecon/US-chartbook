Since the second quarter of 2020, inflation-adjusted US debt increased at an annualized rate of 1.2 percent, far below the long-term rate of 3.7 percent. Over this six-year period, debt growth is driven largely by an increase in federal government debt. Federal government debt contributed 1.1 percentage points to annualized growth, and household debt contributed 0.1 percentage point.
