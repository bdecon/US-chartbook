Nonparticipants usually comprise about half of the population, and total 161.4 million in July 2025. The category includes children (59.2 million), students (12.2 million), unpaid caregivers (12.2 million), those unable to work due to disability or illness (13.8 million), those who want a job but have given up looking (6.5 million), and retirees and the elderly (54.7 million).
