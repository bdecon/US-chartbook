Actual hours worked by people at work during the household survey reference week average 38.3 in September 2025 (see {\color{blue}\textbf{---}}), slightly below the 38.9 hour average during 2017 to 2019. Hours worked average 39.6 from 1998 through 2000, and fell to a great recession low of 37.4 in February 2010.

The CPS-based measure for all workers (see \colordotline{{violet}}) averages 36.9 hours per week in September 2025, following 36.8 in September 2024, and compared to 37.5 hours during 2017--2019.
