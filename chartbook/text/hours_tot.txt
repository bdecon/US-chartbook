Actual hours worked by people at work during the household survey reference week average 38.4 in January 2026 (see {\color{blue}\textbf{---}}), slightly below the 38.9 hour average during 2017 to 2019. Hours worked average 39.6 from 1998 through 2000, and fell to a Great Recession low of 37.4 in February 2010.

The CPS-based measure for all workers (see \colordotline{{violet}}) averages 37.0 hours per week in January 2026, following 36.9 in January 2025, and compared to 37.5 hours during 2017--2019.
