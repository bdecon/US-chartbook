Consumer spending on services other than housing and utilities totals \$32,397 per person, on an annualized basis, in 2026 Q1 (see {\color{blue!75!white}\textbf{---}}), compared to an inflation-adjusted \$32,248 in 2025 Q4, and \$28,605 in 2019 Q4. Spending on non-housing services has increased 13.3 percent since 2019 Q4. 

Shelter costs, which combine housing, utilities, and residential fixed investment, are \$14,820 per person in 2026 Q1 (see {\color{green!85!blue}\textbf{---}}), \$14,847 in 2025 Q4, and \$14,155 in 2019 Q4. Shelter spending peaked at \$16,200 per person in the third quarter of 2005, during the housing bubble.
