Consumer spending on services other than housing and utilities totals \$31,976 per person, on an annualized basis, in 2025 Q4 (see {\color{blue!75!white}\textbf{---}}), compared to an inflation-adjusted \$31,691 in 2025 Q3, and \$28,293 in 2019 Q4. Spending on non-housing services has increased 13 percent since 2019 Q4. 

Shelter costs, which combine housing, utilities, and residential fixed investment, are \$14,720 per person in 2025 Q4 (see {\color{green!85!blue}\textbf{---}}), \$14,688 in 2025 Q3, and \$14,027 in 2019 Q4. Shelter spending peaked at \$16,045 per person in the third quarter of 2005, during the housing bubble.
