Consumer spending on services other than housing and utilities totals \$32,862 per person, on an annualized basis, in 2026 Q2 (see {\color{blue!75!white}\textbf{---}}), compared to an inflation-adjusted \$32,560 in 2026 Q1, and \$28,872 in 2019 Q4. Spending on non-housing services has increased 13.8 percent since 2019 Q4. 

Shelter costs, which combine housing, utilities, and residential fixed investment, are \$14,989 per person in 2026 Q2 (see {\color{green!85!blue}\textbf{---}}), \$14,969 in 2026 Q1, and \$14,311 in 2019 Q4. Shelter spending peaked at \$16,375 per person in the third quarter of 2005, during the housing bubble.
