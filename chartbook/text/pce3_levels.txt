Consumer spending on services other than housing and utilities totals \$31,899 per person, on an annualized basis, in 2025 Q4 (see {\color{blue!75!white}\textbf{---}}), compared to an inflation-adjusted \$31,693 in 2025 Q3, and \$28,295 in 2019 Q4. Spending on non-housing services has increased 12.7 percent since 2019 Q4. 

Shelter costs, which combine housing, utilities, and residential fixed investment, are \$14,741 per person in 2025 Q4 (see {\color{green!85!blue}\textbf{---}}), \$14,699 in 2025 Q3, and \$14,039 in 2019 Q4. Shelter spending peaked at \$16,063 per person in the third quarter of 2005, during the housing bubble.
