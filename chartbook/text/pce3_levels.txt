Consumer spending on services other than housing and utilities totals \$30,796 per person, on an annualized basis, in 2025 Q2 (see {\color{blue!75!white}\textbf{---}}), compared to an inflation-adjusted \$30,564 in 2025 Q1, and \$27,810 in 2019 Q4. Spending on non-housing services has increased 10.7 percent since 2019 Q4. 

Shelter costs, which combine housing, utilities, and residential fixed investment, are \$14,567 per person in 2025 Q2 (see {\color{green!85!blue}\textbf{---}}), \$14,639 in 2025 Q1, and \$13,854 in 2019 Q4. Shelter spending peaked at \$15,856 per person in the third quarter of 2005, during the housing bubble.
