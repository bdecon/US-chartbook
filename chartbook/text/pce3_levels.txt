Consumer spending on services other than housing and utilities totals \$31,375 per person, on an annualized basis, in 2025 Q3 (see {\color{blue!75!white}\textbf{---}}), compared to an inflation-adjusted \$31,052 in 2025 Q2, and \$28,041 in 2019 Q4. Spending on non-housing services has increased 11.9 percent since 2019 Q4. 

Shelter costs, which combine housing, utilities, and residential fixed investment, are \$14,605 per person in 2025 Q3 (see {\color{green!85!blue}\textbf{---}}), \$14,684 in 2025 Q2, and \$13,965 in 2019 Q4. Shelter spending peaked at \$15,985 per person in the third quarter of 2005, during the housing bubble.
