Consumer credit totals \$5.11 trillion on a seasonally adjusted and annualized basis in January 2026. Over the past year, consumer credit increased by 3.2 percent, while after-tax income increased by 4.6 percent. As a result, the ratio of consumer credit to disposable income decreased by a total of 0.3 percentage point.
