Consumer credit totals \$5.19 trillion on a seasonally adjusted basis in July 2026. Over the past year, consumer credit increased by 2.6 percent, while after-tax income increased by 4.2 percent. As a result, the ratio of consumer credit to disposable income decreased by a total of 0.3 percentage point.
