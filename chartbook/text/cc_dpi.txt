Consumer credit totals \$5.14 trillion on a seasonally adjusted and annualized basis in March 2026. Over the past year, consumer credit increased by 2.3 percent, while after-tax income increased by four percent. As a result, the ratio of consumer credit to disposable income decreased by a total of 0.3 percentage point.
