In the monthly measure, consumer credit totals \$5.01 trillion US dollars on a seasonally-adjusted and annualized basis in March 2025. Over the past year, consumer credit decreased by 0.8 percent, while after-tax income increased by four percent. As a result, the ratio of consumer credit to disposable income decreased by a total of 1.1 percentage points. In March 2025, total consumer credit is equivalent to 22.4 percent of annualized March 2025 disposable income (see {\color{blue!50!black}\textbf{---}}).
