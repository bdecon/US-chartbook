In the monthly measure, consumer credit totals \$5.06 trillion on a seasonally-adjusted and annualized basis in July 2025. Over the past year, consumer credit increased by 0.3 percent, while after-tax income increased by 4.6 percent. As a result, the ratio of consumer credit to disposable income decreased by a total of one percentage point. In July 2025, total consumer credit is equivalent to 22.4 percent of annualized July 2025 disposable income (see {\color{blue!50!black}\textbf{---}}).
