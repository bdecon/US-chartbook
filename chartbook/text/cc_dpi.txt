Consumer credit totals \$5.08 trillion on a seasonally-adjusted and annualized basis in October 2025. Over the past year, consumer credit increased by 0.3 percent, while after-tax income increased by 4.8 percent. As a result, the ratio of consumer credit to disposable income decreased by a total of one percentage point. In October 2025, consumer credit equals 22.0 percent of disposable income (see {\color{blue!50!black}\textbf{---}}).
