In December 2025, the median full-time worker receives \$1,195 per week, following \$1,216 per week in November and \$1,208 per week in September. The average over these three months is \$1,207 per week, a 2.4 percent increase over the same three months, one year prior (see {\color{violet!80!blue}\textbf{---}}). 

Median usual weekly earnings increased 1.5 percent over the year ending December 2025 \seebox{violet!80!blue}, following increases of 3.1 percent in November and 2.6 percent in September.
