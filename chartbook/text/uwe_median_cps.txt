In September 2025, the median full-time worker receives \$1,208 per week, following \$1,222 per week in August and \$1,182 per week in July. The average over these three months is \$1,204 per week, a four percent increase over the same three months, one year prior (see {\color{violet!80!blue}\textbf{---}}).
