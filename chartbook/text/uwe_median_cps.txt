In April 2025, the median full-time worker receives \$1,219 per week, following \$1,203 per week in March and \$1,195 per week in February. The average over these three months is \$1,206 per week, a 5.7 percent increase over the same three months, one year prior (see {\color{violet!80!blue}\textbf{---}}). 

Median usual weekly earnings increased 6.6 percent over the year ending April 2025 (see\cbox{violet!80!blue}), following increases of 4.6 percent in March and 5.9 percent in February.
