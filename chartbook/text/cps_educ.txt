Over the year ending September 2025, 22.7 million people over the age of 25, or 39.5 percent of the total, have at least a bachelor's degree, with 8.8 million of those, or 15.2 percent of the total, holding an advanced degree such as a master's degree, medical or law degree, or PhD. 

An additional 14.3 million people have some college coursework but no degree or have an associate degree. A total of 15.8 million have a high school diploma but no college, while 4.8 million have no high school diploma.
