Over the year ending April 2025, 60.1 million people over the age of 25, or 39.2 percent of the total, have at least a bachelor's degree, with 23.2 million of those, or 15.1 percent of the total, holding an advanced degree such as a master's degree, medical or law degree, or PhD. 

An additional 37.8 million people have some college coursework but no degree or have an associate degree. A total of 42.5 million have a high school diploma but no college, while 12.9 million have no high school diploma.
