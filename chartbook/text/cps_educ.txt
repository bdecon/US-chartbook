Over the year ending June 2025, 45.2 million people over the age of 25, or 39.3 percent of the total, have at least a bachelor's degree, with 17.5 million of those, or 15.2 percent of the total, holding an advanced degree such as a master's degree, medical or law degree, or PhD. 

An additional 28.4 million people have some college coursework but no degree or have an associate degree. A total of 31.9 million have a high school diploma but no college, while 9.6 million have no high school diploma.
