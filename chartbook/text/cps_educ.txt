Over the year ending July 2025, 37.7 million people over the age of 25, or 39.3 percent of the total, have at least a bachelor's degree, with 14.6 million of those, or 15.2 percent of the total, holding an advanced degree such as a master's degree, medical or law degree, or PhD. 

An additional 23.7 million people have some college coursework but no degree or have an associate degree. A total of 26.5 million have a high school diploma but no college, while 8.0 million have no high school diploma.
