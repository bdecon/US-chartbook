The ratio of debt service and rent payments to disposable personal income peaked at 18.8 percent in 2007 Q4, during the housing bubble.

As of 2025 Q1, \textbf{debt service and rent} are 14.6 percent of after-tax income (see {\color{blue!80!black}\textbf{---}}), an increase of 0.09 percentage point from the year prior. The ratio fell by 0.39 percentage point since 2019.

In the latest quarter, the ratio of \textbf{debt service} payments to income is 11.2 percent (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}). The debt service ratio fell by 0.48 percentage point since 2019. In 2025 Q1, the ratio of mortage debt service to income is 5.8 percent, and the ratio of consumer credit debt service to income is 5.4 percent.
