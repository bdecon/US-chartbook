The FRBNY data show mortgage debt, including home equity lines of credit, totals \$13.60 trillion in the third quarter of 2025, equivalent to 58.8 percent of disposable personal income. Student loans total \$1,664 billion, or 7.2 percent of income; auto loans total \$1,667 billion (7.2 percent of income); and credit card debt is \$1,277 billion (5.4 percent of income).
