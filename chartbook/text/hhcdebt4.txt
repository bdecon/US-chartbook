The FRBNY data show mortgage debt, including home equity lines of credit, totals \$13.35 trillion in the second quarter of 2025, equivalent to 59.2 percent of disposable personal income. Student loans total \$1,638 billion, or 7.3 percent of income; auto loans total \$1,655 billion (7.3 percent of income); and credit card debt is \$1,209 billion (5.4 percent of income).
