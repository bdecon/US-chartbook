The FRBNY data show mortgage debt, including home equity lines of credit, totals \$13.60 trillion in the fourth quarter of 2025, equivalent to 58.7 percent of disposable personal income. Student loans total \$1,660 billion, or 7.2 percent of income; auto loans total \$1,670 billion (7.2 percent of income); and credit card debt is \$1,280 billion (5.5 percent of income).
