The FRBNY data show mortgage debt, including home equity lines of credit, totals \$13.64 trillion in the first quarter of 2026, equivalent to 58.0 percent of disposable personal income. Student loans total \$1,660 billion, or 7.1 percent of income; auto loans total \$1,680 billion (7.1 percent of income); and credit card debt is \$1,250 billion (5.3 percent of income).
