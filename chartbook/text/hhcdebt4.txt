The FRBNY data show mortgage debt, including home equity lines of credit, totals \$13.21 trillion in the first quarter of 2025, equivalent to 59.3 percent of disposable personal income. Student loans total \$1,631 billion, or 7.3 percent of income; auto loans total \$1,642 billion (7.4 percent of income); and credit card debt is \$1,182 billion (5.3 percent of income).
