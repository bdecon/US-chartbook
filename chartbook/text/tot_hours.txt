Hours rise in expansions and fall during recessions. Comparing two recent expansions, from 1994 to 1999, aggregate hours increased by 2.5 percent per year; from 2014 to 2019, hours increased 1.7 percent per year.

In 2025, hours worked increased 0.3 percent, following virtually no change in 2024. Hours increased at an annual rate of 0.4 percent, year to date, in 2026.
