BLS \href{https://www.bls.gov/webapps/legacy/cpswktab5.htm}{report} first decile usual earnings for full-time workers of \$619 per week in 2025 Q1 and \$594 per week in 2024 Q1, a nominal one-year increase of 4.2 percent (see {\color{blue!65!black}\textbf{---}}). Over the year ending 2024 Q4, first decile usual weekly earnings increased 3.6 percent.

The more-volatile CPS-based monthly measure shows first decile usual earnings of \$612 per week in June 2025, \$615 per week in May 2025, and \$620 per week in April 2025. The three-month average is \$615 per week; first decile earnings increased 3.2 percent over the same months, one year prior (see {\color{lime!65!green!90!black}\textbf{---}}). By month, over the year ending June 2025, first decile earnings increased 1.7 percent, following increases of 4.8 percent in May and 3.2 percent in April.
