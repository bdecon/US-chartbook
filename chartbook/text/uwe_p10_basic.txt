BLS \href{https://www.bls.gov/webapps/legacy/cpswktab5.htm}{reports} first decile usual earnings for full-time workers of \$642 per week in 2026 Q2 and \$615 per week in 2025 Q2, a nominal one-year increase of 4.4 percent (see {\color{steelblue}\textbf{---}}). Over the year ending 2026 Q1, first decile usual weekly earnings increased 0.6 percent.

The more volatile CPS-based monthly measure shows first decile usual earnings of \$623 per week in September 2026, \$630 per week in August 2026, and \$628 per week in July 2026. The three-month average is \$627 per week; first decile earnings increased 2.4 percent over the same months, one year prior (see {\color{deepgreen}\textbf{---}}). By month, over the year ending September 2026, first decile earnings increased 1.2 percent, following increases of 3.4 percent in August and 2.6 percent in July.
