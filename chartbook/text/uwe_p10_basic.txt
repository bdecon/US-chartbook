BLS \href{https://www.bls.gov/webapps/legacy/cpswktab5.htm}{reports} first decile usual earnings for full-time workers of \$616 per week in 2025 Q3 and \$607 per week in 2024 Q3, a nominal one-year increase of 1.5 percent (see {\color{blue!65!black}\textbf{---}}). Over the year ending 2025 Q2, first decile usual weekly earnings increased 3.2 percent.

The more volatile CPS-based monthly measure shows first decile usual earnings of \$637 per week in February 2026, \$625 per week in January 2026, and \$620 per week in December 2025. The three-month average is \$627 per week; first decile earnings increased 1.4 percent over the same months, one year prior (see {\color{lime!65!green!90!black}\textbf{---}}). By month, over the year ending February 2026, first decile earnings increased 1.3 percent, following increases of 0.8 percent in January and 2.1 percent in December 2025.
