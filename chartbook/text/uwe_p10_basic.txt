BLS \href{https://www.bls.gov/webapps/legacy/cpswktab5.htm}{report} first decile usual earnings for full-time workers of \$619 per week in 2025 Q1 and \$594 per week in 2024 Q1, a nominal one-year increase of 4.2 percent (see {\color{blue!65!black}\textbf{---}}). Over the year ending 2024 Q4, first decile usual weekly earnings increased 3.6 percent.

The more-volatile CPS-based monthly measure shows first decile usual earnings of \$620 per week in April 2025, \$614 per week in March 2025, and \$635 per week in February 2025. The three-month average is \$623 per week; first decile earnings increased 4.4 percent over the same months, one year prior (see {\color{lime!65!green!90!black}\textbf{---}}). By month, over the year ending April 2025, first decile earnings increased 3.2 percent, following increases of three percent in March and 7.2 percent in February.
