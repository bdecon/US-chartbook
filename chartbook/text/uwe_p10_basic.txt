BLS \href{https://www.bls.gov/webapps/legacy/cpswktab5.htm}{report} first decile usual earnings for full-time workers of \$615 per week in 2025 Q2 and \$596 per week in 2024 Q2, a nominal one-year increase of 3.2 percent (see {\color{blue!65!black}\textbf{---}}). Over the year ending 2025 Q1, first decile usual weekly earnings increased 4.2 percent.

The more-volatile CPS-based monthly measure shows first decile usual earnings of \$610 per week in July 2025, \$612 per week in June 2025, and \$615 per week in May 2025. The three-month average is \$612 per week; first decile earnings increased 2.2 percent over the same months, one year prior (see {\color{lime!65!green!90!black}\textbf{---}}). By month, over the year ending July 2025, first decile earnings increased 0.1 percent, following increases of 1.7 percent in June and 4.8 percent in May.
