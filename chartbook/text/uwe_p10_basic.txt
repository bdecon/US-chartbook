BLS \href{https://www.bls.gov/webapps/legacy/cpswktab5.htm}{report} first decile usual earnings for full-time workers of \$616 per week in 2025 Q3 and \$607 per week in 2024 Q3, a nominal one-year increase of 1.5 percent (see {\color{blue!65!black}\textbf{---}}). Over the year ending 2025 Q2, first decile usual weekly earnings increased 3.2 percent.

The more-volatile CPS-based monthly measure shows first decile usual earnings of \$617 per week in September 2025, \$616 per week in August 2025, and \$610 per week in July 2025. The three-month average is \$614 per week; first decile earnings increased 0.9 percent over the same months, one year prior (see {\color{lime!65!green!90!black}\textbf{---}}). By month, over the year ending September 2025, first decile earnings increased 2.8 percent, following a decrease of 0.2 percent in August, and an increase of 0.1 percent in July.
