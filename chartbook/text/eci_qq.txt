In 2025 Q2, wages increased four percent, following increases of 3.1 percent in Q1 and 3.8 percent in 2024 Q4 \seebox[gray]{cyan!50!lime!50!white}. Growth is currently below the 2021 highs and above the pre-COVID average.

Adjusted for inflation using the PCE deflator, growth is 1.9 percent in 2025 Q2, following a decrease of 0.3 percent in Q1, and an increase of 1.3 percent in 2024 Q4 (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}). Real wage growth is above the long-term average.
