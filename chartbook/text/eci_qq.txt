In 2025 Q2, wages increased four percent, following increases of 3.1 percent in Q1 and 3.8 percent in 2024 Q4 (see\cbox{cyan!50!lime!70!white}). Growth is currently below the 2021 highs and above the pre-COVID average.

Adjusted for inflation using the PCE deflator, growth is two percent in 2025 Q2, following a decrease of 0.5 percent in Q1, and an increase of 1.5 percent in 2024 Q4 (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}). Real wage growth is above the long-term average.
