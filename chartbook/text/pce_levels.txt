Total consumer spending is \$20.5 trillion in 2025 Q1, compared to a price-adjusted \$20.4 trillion in 2024 Q4 and \$17.7 trillion in 2019 Q4. On a per person basis, consumer spending is \$59,901 in 2025 Q1, of which \$18,710 are spent on goods (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}) and \$41,191 on services (see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}).
