Consumer spending totals \$21.7 trillion in 2026 Q1, compared to a price-adjusted \$21.6 trillion in 2025 Q4 and \$18.3 trillion in 2019 Q4.

On a per person basis, consumer spending is \$63,295 in 2026 Q1, of which \$19,463 are spent on goods (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}) and \$43,832 on services (see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}). In the fourth quarter of 2019, before the pandemic, consumer spending on goods was \$16,238 per person, and spending on services was \$39,090 per person, after adjusting for inflation.
