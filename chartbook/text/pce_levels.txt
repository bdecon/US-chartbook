Consumer spending totals \$21.4 trillion in 2025 Q4, compared to a price-adjusted \$21.3 trillion in 2025 Q3 and \$18.1 trillion in 2019 Q4. On a per person basis, consumer spending is \$62,401 in 2025 Q4, of which \$19,227 are spent on goods (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}) and \$43,174 on services (see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}). In the fourth quarter of 2019, before the pandemic, consumer spending on goods was \$16,027 per person, and spending on services was \$38,709 per person, after adjusting for inflation.
