Consumer spending totals \$21.1 trillion in 2025 Q3, compared to a price-adjusted \$20.9 trillion in 2025 Q2 and \$18.0 trillion in 2019 Q4. On a per person basis, consumer spending is \$61,629 in 2025 Q3, of which \$19,108 are spent on goods (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}) and \$42,520 on services (see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}). In the fourth quarter of 2019, before the pandemic, consumer spending on goods was \$15,948 per person, and spending on services was \$38,391 per person, after adjusting for inflation.
