Consumer spending totals \$20.8 trillion in 2025 Q2, compared to a price-adjusted \$20.7 trillion in 2025 Q1 and \$17.9 trillion in 2019 Q4. On a per person basis, consumer spending is \$60,783 in 2025 Q2, of which \$18,919 are spent on goods (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}) and \$41,864 on services (see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}). In the fourth quarter of 2019, before the pandemic, consumer spending on goods was \$15,885 per person, and spending on services was \$38,080 per person, after adjusting for inflation.
