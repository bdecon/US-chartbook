In the second quarter of 2025, median usual earnings of full-time wage and salary workers are \$1,196 per week, compared to \$1,143 per week in 2024 Q2, a nominal one-year increase of 4.6 percent (see {\color{cyan!60!white}\textbf{---}}). In 2025 Q1, the median full-time worker receives \$1,194 per week, a one-year increase of 4.8 percent.
