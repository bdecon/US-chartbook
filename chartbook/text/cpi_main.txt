The Consumer Price Index (CPI) \href{https://www.bls.gov/cpi/}{measures} changes in the price of goods and services purchased by urban households. The one-year change in the CPI is the most popular measure of inflation. 

Consumer prices increased 4.2 percent over the year ending May 2026 (see {\color{blue!60!cyan}\textbf{---}}), according to the CPI. The core CPI, which does not include the more volatile food and energy prices, increased 2.9 percent over the same period (see {\color{gray}\textbf{---}}).
