In September 2025, the US utilizes 75.9 percent of total industrial capacity (see {\color{blue!80!black}\textbf{---}}), and 75.5 percent of manufacturing capacity (see {\color{blue!40!cyan}\textbf{---}}). In 2019, the total capacity utilization rate averaged 77.9 percent, and the manufacturing capacity utilization rate averaged 77.4 percent. Total capacity utilization has decreased by two percentage points since 2019, and decreased by 7.9 percentage points since 1989.
