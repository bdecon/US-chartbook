In May 2025, the US is utilizing 77.4 percent of total industrial capacity (see {\color{blue!80!black}\textbf{---}}), and 76.7 percent of manufacturing capacity (see {\color{blue!40!cyan}\textbf{---}}). In 2019, the total capacity utilization rate averaged 78.6 percent, and the manufacturing capacity utilization rate averaged 77.2 percent. Total capacity utilization has decreased by 1.2 percentage points since 2019, and decreased by 6.3 percentage points since 1989.
