In 2024, labor productivity increased by 2.7 percent (see \tikz[baseline=-1mm] \draw[black, fill=white] (0pt,0pt) circle (1.5pt);). Capital intensity contributed 1.1 percentage points (see\hspace{-0.8mm}\cbox{violet!60!white}\hspace{-0.5mm}), and labor composition contributed 0.3 percentage point (see\hspace{-0.8mm}\cbox{blue!60!black}\hspace{-0.5mm}). The remainder, called total factor productivity, added 1.3 percentage points (see\hspace{-0.8mm}\cbox{cyan!40!white}\hspace{-0.5mm}).
