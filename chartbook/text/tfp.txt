In 2025, labor productivity increased by 2.2 percent (see \tikz[baseline=-1mm] \draw[black, fill=white] (0pt,0pt) circle (1.5pt);). Capital intensity contributed 0.9 percentage point \seebox{violet!60!white}, and labor composition contributed 0.4 percentage point \seebox{steelblue}. The remainder, called total factor productivity, added 0.8 percentage point \seebox{cyan!40!white}.
