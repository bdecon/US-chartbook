In 2024, labor productivity increased by 2.9 percent (see \tikz[baseline=-1mm] \draw[black, fill=white] (0pt,0pt) circle (1.5pt);). Capital intensity contributed 1.1 percentage points \seebox{violet!60!white}, and labor composition contributed 0.3 percentage point \seebox{steelblue}. The remainder, called total factor productivity, added 1.5 percentage points \seebox{cyan!40!white}.
