In 2024, among commuter zones with a population of at least 100,000, the commuter zone (listed by largest city) with the highest share of its population under 18 is Laredo, TX (30.5 percent), followed by Provo, UT (30.3 percent), and Odessa, TX (29.5 percent). The commuter zones with the lowest share of the local population under 18 were Port Angeles, WA (13.7 percent), Sarasota, FL (14.9 percent), and Pittsfield, MA (15.5 percent).
