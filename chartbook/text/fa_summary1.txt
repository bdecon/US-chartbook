Private households control most of the US economy, with assets equal to 647.1 percent of GDP in 2025 Q2, and wealth equal to 578.3 percent of GDP. Both increased considerably since 2000. Private nonfinancial businesses hold 305.0 percent of GDP in assets and 142.3 percent of GDP in liabilities, with equity of 162.7 percent of GDP.

Government assets, excluding land, are 104.3 percent of GDP, while liabilities are 138.2 percent of GDP. The US holds 118.8 percent of GDP in foreign financial assets, while the rest of the world has 198.9 percent of GDP in financial claims on the US.
