Private households control most of the US economy, with assets equal to 668.7 percent of GDP in 2026 Q2, and wealth equal to 601.5 percent of GDP. Both have increased considerably since 2000. Private nonfinancial businesses hold 311.7 percent of GDP in assets and 140.4 percent of GDP in liabilities, with equity of 171.3 percent of GDP.

Government assets, excluding land, are 105.9 percent of GDP, while liabilities are 137.8 percent of GDP. The US holds 132.5 percent of GDP in foreign financial assets, while the rest of the world has 196.2 percent of GDP in financial claims on the US.
