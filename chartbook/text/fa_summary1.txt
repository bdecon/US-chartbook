Households control most of the US economy, with assets equal to 634.4 percent of GDP in 2025 Q1, and wealth equal to 565.1 percent of GDP. Both increased considerably since 2000. Private nonfinancial businesses hold 307.3 percent of GDP in assets and 142.9 percent of GDP in liabilities, with equity of 164.4 percent of GDP.

Government assets, excluding land, are 104.9 percent of GDP, while liabilities are 140.8 percent of GDP. The US holds 112.9 percent of GDP in foreign financial assets, while the rest of the world has 188.3 percent of GDP in financial claims on the US.
