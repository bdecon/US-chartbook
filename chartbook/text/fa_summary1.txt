Private households control most of the US economy, with assets equal to 653.9 percent of GDP in 2025 Q4, and wealth equal to 585.5 percent of GDP. Both have increased considerably since 2000. Private nonfinancial businesses hold 307.4 percent of GDP in assets and 139.2 percent of GDP in liabilities, with equity of 168.2 percent of GDP.

Government assets, excluding land, are 105.8 percent of GDP, while liabilities are 139.3 percent of GDP. The US holds 125.1 percent of GDP in foreign financial assets, while the rest of the world has 208.2 percent of GDP in financial claims on the US.
