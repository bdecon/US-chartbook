Private households control most of the US economy, with assets equal to 642.8 percent of GDP in 2026 Q1, and wealth equal to 575.1 percent of GDP. Both have increased considerably since 2000. Private nonfinancial businesses hold 315.5 percent of GDP in assets and 143.4 percent of GDP in liabilities, with equity of 172.2 percent of GDP.

Government assets, excluding land, are 106.7 percent of GDP, while liabilities are 140.6 percent of GDP. The US holds 126.4 percent of GDP in foreign financial assets, while the rest of the world has 204.8 percent of GDP in financial claims on the US.
