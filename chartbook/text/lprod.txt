In 2025 Q4, nonfarm business labor productivity increased at an annual rate of 1.8 percent \seebox{teal}, as the result of an increase of 1.5 percent in real output and a decrease of 0.2 percent in hours worked. In the prior quarter, 2025 Q3, labor productivity increased at an annual rate of 5.2 percent, as real output increased 5.4 percent and hours of work increased 0.2 percent. Productivity has increased at an annual rate of 1.5 percent over the past five years, substantially below the 1989-onward rate of two percent.
