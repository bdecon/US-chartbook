In 2025 Q1, nonfarm business labor productivity decreased at an annual rate of 0.8 percent (see\cbox{teal}), as the result of a decrease of 0.3 percent in real output and an increase of 0.6 percent in hours worked. In the prior quarter, 2024 Q4, labor productivity increased at an annual rate of 1.7 percent, as real output increased 2.5 percent and hours of work increased 0.8 percent. Productivity has increased at an annual rate of 1.1 percent over the past five years, substantially below the 1989-onward rate of two percent.
