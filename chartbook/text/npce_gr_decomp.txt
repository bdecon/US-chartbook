Since 1989, total US consumer spending increased at an annual rate of 5.1 percent. Real per capita spending increased by 1.8 percent per year, inflation added 2.3 percentage points, and population growth added 0.9 percentage point. In the latest full year of data, 2024, nominal consumer spending growth is 5.6 percent, real per capita growth is two percent, inflation is 2.7 percent, and population growth is 0.9 percent.
