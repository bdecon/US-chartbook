Housing prices increased four percent over the year ending April 2025, substantially above the pre-COVID rate of 2.9 percent (the average monthly rate during 2019). Medical care prices increased 2.7 percent, these prices grew at an average rate of 2.8 percent during 2019. In contrast, prices of food consumed at home (groceries) increased two percent in the year ending April 2025 compared to 0.9 percent during 2019.

Transportation prices decreased 1.5 percent over the year ending April 2025, substantially below the pre-COVID 0.3 percent decrease. Energy prices decreased 3.7 percent over the year, compared to an average 2.1 percent decrease on average in 2019. Energy prices are historically more volatile than other categories.
