In April 2026, 4.9 million people worked part-time for economic reasons, equivalent to 2.9 percent of the labor force (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}). In 2019, an average of 2.7 percent of the labor force worked part-time for economic reasons. In 2010, following the Great Recession, the rate was 5.8 percent.

Voluntary part-time workers total 22.7 million in April 2026, or 13.4 percent of the labor force (see {\color{amber}\textbf{---}}). The category was 13.1 percent of the labor force in 2019, on average.
