The \textbf{inventory} of new homes for sale affects housing prices. The Census Bureau \href{https://www.census.gov/construction/nrs/index.html}{report} a seasonally-adjusted total of 490,000 new houses for sale in August 2025, an increase of 19,000 since August 2024.  At the current pace of new home sales, it would take 7.4 months to exhaust the supply of unsold homes (see {\color{red!90!black}\textbf{---}}). Current inventory levels are in line with the year-prior supply of 8.2 months and slightly above the long-term average supply of six months.
