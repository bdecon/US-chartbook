The \textbf{inventory} of new homes for sale affects housing prices. The Census Bureau \href{https://www.census.gov/construction/nrs/index.html}{reports} a seasonally adjusted total of 483,000 new houses for sale in August 2026, a decrease of 10,000 since August 2025.  At the current pace of new home sales, it would take 8.5 months to exhaust the supply of unsold homes (see {\color{darkred}\textbf{---}}). The current months of supply is in line with the year-prior level of 8.5 months and substantially above the long-term average supply of 6.1 months.
