The \textbf{inventory} of new homes for sale affects housing prices. The Census Bureau \href{https://www.census.gov/construction/nrs/index.html}{report} a seasonally-adjusted total of 503,000 new houses for sale in March 2025, an increase of 37,000 since March 2024.  At the current pace of new home sales, it would take 8.3 months to exhaust the supply of unsold homes (see {\color{red!90!black}\textbf{---}}). Current inventory levels are in line with the year-prior supply of 8.2 months and substantially above the long-term average supply of 6.0 months.
