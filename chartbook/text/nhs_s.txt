The \textbf{inventory} of new homes for sale affects housing prices. The Census Bureau \href{https://www.census.gov/construction/nrs/index.html}{reports} a seasonally adjusted total of 481,000 new houses for sale in March 2026, a decrease of 23,000 since March 2025.  At the current pace of new home sales, it would take 8.5 months to exhaust the supply of unsold homes (see {\color{darkred}\textbf{---}}). The current months of supply is slightly below the year-prior level of 9.2 months and substantially above the long-term average supply of six months.
