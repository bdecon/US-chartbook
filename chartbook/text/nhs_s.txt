The \textbf{inventory} of new homes for sale affects housing prices. The Census Bureau \href{https://www.census.gov/construction/nrs/index.html}{reports} a seasonally adjusted total of 476,000 new houses for sale in January 2026, a decrease of 20,000 since January 2025.  At the current pace of new home sales, it would take 9.7 months to exhaust the supply of unsold homes (see {\color{red!90!black}\textbf{---}}). The current months of supply is in line with the year-prior level of 9.0 months and far above the long-term average supply of six months.
