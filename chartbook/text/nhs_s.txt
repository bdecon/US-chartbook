The \textbf{inventory} of new homes for sale affects housing prices. The Census Bureau \href{https://www.census.gov/construction/nrs/index.html}{report} a seasonally-adjusted total of 499,000 new houses for sale in July 2025, an increase of 34,000 since July 2024.  At the current pace of new home sales, it would take 9.2 months to exhaust the supply of unsold homes (see {\color{red!90!black}\textbf{---}}). Current inventory levels are slightly above the year-prior supply of 7.9 months and far above the long-term average supply of six months.
