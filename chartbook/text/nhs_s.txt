The \textbf{inventory} of new homes for sale affects housing prices. The Census Bureau \href{https://www.census.gov/construction/nrs/index.html}{reports} a seasonally-adjusted total of 488,000 new houses for sale in October 2025, an increase of 8,000 since October 2024.  At the current pace of new home sales, it would take 7.9 months to exhaust the supply of unsold homes (see {\color{red!90!black}\textbf{---}}). The current months of supply is slightly below the year-prior level of 9.3 months and substantially above the long-term average supply of six months.
