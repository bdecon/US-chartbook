In April 2025, seasonally-adjusted civilian employment increased by 436,000 (see\cbox{magenta}), far above the 2019 average increase of 169,000 jobs per month. The US added a net total of 177,000 nonfarm payroll jobs in April 2025 (see\cbox{blue!70!white}), compared to a monthly average of 165,500 in 2019. The average of both surveys over the past three months shows an increase of 85,500 employees per month.
