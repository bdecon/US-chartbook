In July 2025, seasonally-adjusted civilian employment decreased by 260,000 (see\cbox{magenta}), far below the 2019 average increase of 169,000 jobs per month. The US added a net total of 73,000 nonfarm payroll jobs in July 2025 (see\cbox{blue!70!white}), compared to a monthly average of 165,500 in 2019. The average of both surveys over the past three months shows a decrease of 126,200 employees per month.
