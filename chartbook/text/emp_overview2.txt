In August 2025, seasonally-adjusted civilian employment increased by 288,000 \seebox{green!50!teal!85!blue}, substantially above the 2019 average increase of 169,000 jobs per month. The US added a net total of 22,000 nonfarm payroll jobs in August 2025 \seebox{blue!70!white}, compared to a monthly average of 165,500 in 2019. The average of both surveys over the past three months shows an increase of 34,800 employees per month.
