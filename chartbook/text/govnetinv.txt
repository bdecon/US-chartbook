Government gross investment, less depreciation, is the government's net investment in the tangible assets that make the economy more productive. Government investment includes infrastructure, buildings, equipment, intellectual property, and other capital goods.

In the latest data, covering 2025 Q2, annualized government net investment is \$293.1 billion, the result of gross investment of \$1,135.0 billion and \$841.9 billion in depreciation. Government net investment is equivalent to 0.97 percent of GDP in 2025 Q2 (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}), compared to 0.89 percent in 2024 Q2, and 0.72 percent in 2023 Q2.
