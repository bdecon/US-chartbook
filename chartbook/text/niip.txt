In 2025 Q3, domestic holdings of foreign assets total \$41.3 trillion, or 132.7 percent of GDP (see {\color{blue!75!green}\textbf{---}}). These assets translate to 129.8 percent of GDP in 2025 Q2, and 128.7 percent in 2019. Domestic liabilities to the foreign sector total \$68.9 trillion, or 221.5 percent of GDP, in 2025 Q3, following 215.6 percent in 2025 Q2, and 180 percent in 2019 (see {\color{violet!60!black}\textbf{---}}).

The overall result of these financial positions, net IIP, or holdings of foreign assets minus liabilities, identifies the US as a net debtor to the rest of the world, to the equivalent of 88.8 percent of GDP in 2025 Q3, following 85.8 percent in 2025 Q2, and 51.4 percent in 2019 \seebox{cyan!18!white}.
