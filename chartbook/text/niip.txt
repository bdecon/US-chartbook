In 2026 Q1, domestic holdings of foreign assets total \$43.4 trillion, or 136.1 percent of GDP (see {\color{blue!75!green}\textbf{---}}). These assets translate to 136.6 percent of GDP in 2025 Q4, and 128.7 percent in 2019. Domestic liabilities to the foreign sector total \$64.6 trillion, or 202.9 percent of GDP, in 2026 Q1, following 206.2 percent in 2025 Q4, and 180 percent in 2019 (see {\color{violet!60!black}\textbf{---}}).

The overall result of these financial positions, net IIP, or holdings of foreign assets minus liabilities, identifies the US as a net debtor to the rest of the world, to the equivalent of 66.7 percent of GDP in 2026 Q1, following 69.6 percent in 2025 Q4, and 51.4 percent in 2019 \seebox{cyan!18!white}.
