In 2025 Q1, domestic holdings of foreign assets total \$36.9 trillion, or 123 percent of GDP (see {\color{blue!75!green}\textbf{---}}). These assets translate to 120.2 percent of GDP in 2024 Q4, and 128.7 percent in 2019. Domestic liabilities to the foreign sector total \$61.5 trillion, or 205.1 percent of GDP, in 2025 Q1, following 209.5 percent in 2024 Q4, and 180 percent in 2019 (see {\color{violet!60!black}\textbf{---}}).

The overall result of these financial positions, net IIP, or holdings of foreign assets minus liabilities, identifies the US as a net debtor to the rest of the world, to the equivalent of 82.2 percent of GDP in 2025 Q1, following 89.3 percent in 2024 Q4, and 51.4 percent in 2019 \seebox{cyan!18!white}.
