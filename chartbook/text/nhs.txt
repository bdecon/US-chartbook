In January 2026, seasonally adjusted \textbf{annualized sales} of new single-family homes total 587,000 (see {\color{tealgreen}\textbf{---}}), as \href{https://www.census.gov/construction/nrs/pdf/newressales.pdf}{reported} by the Census Bureau. Over the past year, new home sales decreased 0.1 percent. Pre-COVID, in February 2020, the annualized rate of single-family new home sales was 707,000. Since February 2020, new home sales have decreased 17.0 percent.
