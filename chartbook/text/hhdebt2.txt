The ratio of household and nonprofit debt to disposable personal income has fallen to 92.8 percent in 2025 Q4 from the housing bubble peak of 136.8 percent in 2007.

Since 2019, household and nonprofit debt increased 35 percent while disposable personal income increased 44.9 percent. As a result, the debt-to-income ratio fell by 6.8 points. Over the past year, the ratio fell by 0.9 point.
