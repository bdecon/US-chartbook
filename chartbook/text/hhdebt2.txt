The ratio of household and nonprofit debt to disposable personal income has fallen to 92.1 percent in 2026 Q1 from the housing bubble peak of 136.8 percent in 2007.

Since 2019, household and nonprofit debt increased 30.4 percent while disposable personal income increased 43.1 percent. As a result, the debt-to-income ratio fell by 8.9 points. Over the past year, the ratio fell by 0.4 point.
