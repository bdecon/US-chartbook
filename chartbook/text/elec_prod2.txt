During the year ending June 2026, the US generated 4,572 TWh of electricity. By source, 1,822 TWh were generated using natural gas \seebox{purple!80!violet!65!white}, 697 TWh from coal \seebox{gray!70!black}, 791 TWh from nuclear \seebox{cyan!30!white}, and 1,228 TWh from renewable sources \seebox{green!85!darkblue}. Over the past year, renewables increased by 119 TWh, nuclear increased by 15 TWh, and natural gas decreased by 9 TWh.
