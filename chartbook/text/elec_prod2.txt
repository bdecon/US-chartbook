During the year ending July 2026, the US generated 4,581 TWh of electricity. By source, 1,826 TWh were generated using natural gas \seebox{purple!80!violet!65!white}, 693 TWh from coal \seebox{gray!70!black}, 792 TWh from nuclear \seebox{cyan!30!white}, and 1,238 TWh from renewable sources \seebox{green!85!darkblue}. Over the past year, renewables increased by 116 TWh, coal decreased by 19 TWh, and nuclear increased by 14 TWh.
