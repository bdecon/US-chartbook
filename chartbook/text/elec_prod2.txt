During the year ending December 2025, the US generated 4,523 TWh of electricity. By source, 1,807 TWh were generated using natural gas \seebox{purple!80!violet!65!white}, 737 TWh from coal \seebox{gray!70!black}, 785 TWh from nuclear \seebox{cyan!30!white}, and 1,162 TWh from renewable sources \seebox{green!85!darkblue}. Over the past year, renewables increased by 102 TWh, coal increased by 85 TWh, and natural gas decreased by 63 TWh.
