During the year ending March 2026, the US generated 4,541 TWh of electricity. By source, 1,812 TWh were generated using natural gas \seebox{purple!80!violet!65!white}, 715 TWh from coal \seebox{gray!70!black}, 786 TWh from nuclear \seebox{cyan!30!white}, and 1,194 TWh from renewable sources \seebox{green!85!darkblue}. Over the past year, renewables increased by 104 TWh, natural gas decreased by 40 TWh, and coal increased by 26 TWh.
