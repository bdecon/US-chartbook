During the year ending February 2026, the US generated 4,525 TWh of electricity. By source, 1,803 TWh were generated using natural gas \seebox{purple!80!violet!65!white}, 720 TWh from coal \seebox{gray!70!black}, 787 TWh from nuclear \seebox{cyan!30!white}, and 1,181 TWh from renewable sources \seebox{green!85!darkblue}. Over the past year, renewables increased by 105 TWh, natural gas decreased by 62 TWh, and coal increased by 42 TWh.
