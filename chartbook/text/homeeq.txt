As of 2025 Q2, the Federal Reserve \href{https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/z1/20191212/html/b101.htm}{report} \textbf{owners' equity} is 72.6 percent of residential real estate (see {\color{blue!80!violet}\textbf{---}}). Over the past three years, the owners' equity share was virtually unchanged.

Over the past year, the share decreased by a total of 0.5 percentage point. The current share is substantially above the 1989 average of 67.9 percent.
