Over the year ending April 2026, 8.4 percent of 25- to 54-year-olds who were out of the labor force due to disability or illness one year prior became employed (see {\color{blue}\textbf{---}}).

In 2019, eight percent of those in the category found a job. The one-year job-finding rate rebounded from a 2010--2013 average of 5.8 percent.

For those age 16 and older, the comparable rate is 6.1 percent in April 2026 (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}), and 5.5 percent in 2019.
