As of 2026 Q2, \textbf{nonfinancial business debt}--the debt security and loan liabilities of nonfinancial private businesses--totals \$24 trillion. Of this, \$15.7 trillion, or 65.5 percent of the total, is held by corporate businesses. The remaining \$8.3 trillion is held by noncorporate businesses.

Over the past three years, nonfinancial business debt has fallen relative to overall economic activity. As a share of GDP, nonfinancial business debt fell by 4.3 percentage points to 73.8 percent in 2026 Q2 from 78.1 percent in 2023 Q2. The overall change was partially driven by a decrease of 0.5 percent of GDP in nonbank loans \seebox{blue!70!white}.
