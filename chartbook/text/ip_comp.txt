As of August 2026, of a subset of 12 industries that contribute the majority of industrial production, six increased \textbf{production} since February 2020, and six decreased production \seebox{black!20}.

Since February 2020, production of computer and electronic products increased by 27.5 percent, aerospace and miscellaneous transportation equipment production increased by 22.6 percent, paper production decreased by 21.6 percent, and electric and gas utilities production increased by 10.9 percent. 

Since February 2020, six of the 12 industries increased \textbf{capacity}, five decreased capacity, and one was virtually unchanged \seebox{cyan!80!blue}. Production capacity for computer and electronic products increased by 24.0 percent, electric and gas utilities capacity increased by 19.2 percent, and motor vehicles and parts capacity increased by 13.2 percent.
