As of April 2025, of a subset of 12 industries that contribute the majority of industrial production, six increased \textbf{production} since February 2020, and six decreased production (see\cbox{black!20}).

Since February 2020, aerospace and miscellaneous transportation equipment production increased by 17.6 percent, paper production decreased by 15.4 percent, production of computer and electronic products increased by 13.8 percent, and chemical production increased by 13.7 percent. 

Since February 2020, six of the 12 industries increased \textbf{capacity}, six decreased capacity, and none were unchanged (see\cbox{cyan!80!blue}). Production capacity for computer and electronic products increased by 16.7 percent, production capacity for plastics and rubber products increased by 15.3 percent, and electric and gas utilities capacity increased by 15.1 percent.
