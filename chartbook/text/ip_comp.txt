As of January 2026, of a subset of 12 industries that contribute the majority of industrial production, five increased \textbf{production} since February 2020, and seven decreased production \seebox{black!20}.

Since February 2020, paper production decreased by 21.6 percent, production of computer and electronic products increased by 21.5 percent, aerospace and miscellaneous transportation equipment production increased by 20.2 percent, and electric and gas utilities production increased by 11.7 percent. 

Since February 2020, seven of the 12 industries increased \textbf{capacity}, five decreased capacity, and none were virtually unchanged \seebox{cyan!80!blue}. Production capacity for computer and electronic products increased by 19.6 percent, electric and gas utilities capacity increased by 17.4 percent, and motor vehicles and parts capacity increased by 12.8 percent.
