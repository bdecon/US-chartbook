As of September 2025, of a subset of 12 industries that contribute the majority of industrial production, five increased \textbf{production} since February 2020, six decreased production, and one was unchanged \seebox{black!20}.

Since February 2020, paper production decreased by 19.5 percent, aerospace and miscellaneous transportation equipment production increased by 18.6 percent, production of computer and electronic products increased by 18.0 percent, and production of plastics and rubber products decreased by 7.3 percent. 

Since February 2020, seven of the 12 industries increased \textbf{capacity}, five decreased capacity, and none were virtually unchanged \seebox{cyan!80!blue}. Production capacity for computer and electronic products increased by 16.8 percent, electric and gas utilities capacity increased by 15.9 percent, and motor vehicles and parts capacity increased by 13.2 percent.
