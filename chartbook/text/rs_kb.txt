Nonstore sales were 1.4 percent of after-tax income in January 1992 and 6.7 percent in September 2025, a shift that is equivalent to \$1.2 trillion per year. Since 1992, sales as a share of after-tax income have decreased in food and beverage stores (-3.2 percentage points), motor vehicles and parts stores (-1.1 percentage points), and general merchandise stores (-1.1 percentage points).
