Nonstore sales were 1.4 percent of after-tax income in January 1992 and 6.1 percent in July 2026, a shift that is equivalent to \$1.1 trillion per year. Since 1992, sales as a share of after-tax income have decreased in food and beverage stores (-3.4 percentage points), motor vehicles and parts stores (-1.4 percentage points), and clothing and accessories stores (-1.1 percentage points).
