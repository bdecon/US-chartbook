Nonstore sales were 1.4 percent of after-tax income in January 1992 and 6.7 percent in May 2025, a shift that is equivalent to \$1,186 billion per year. Since 1992, sales as a share of after-tax income has decreased in food and beverage stores (-3.2 percentage points), motor vehicles and parts stores (-1.2 percentage points), and clothing and accessories stores (-1.0 percentage point).
