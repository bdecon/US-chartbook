In the national accounts, disposable personal income, or \textbf{after-tax income}, totals \$23.2 trillion, on an annualized basis, in December 2025, equivalent to \$67,705 per person (see {\color{blue!75!black}\textbf{---}}). Personal consumption expenditures, or \textbf{consumer spending}, totals \$21.5 trillion in December 2025, or \$62,701 per person (see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}). \textbf{Personal saving}, calculated as after-tax income minus consumer spending and other outlays such as interest payments, totals \$0.83 trillion, or \$2,426 per person (see {\color{green!80!blue}\textbf{---}}).
