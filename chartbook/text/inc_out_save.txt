In the national accounts, disposable personal income, or \textbf{after-tax income}, totals \$23.5 trillion, on an annualized basis, in April 2026, equivalent to \$68,496 per person (see {\color{navyblue}\textbf{---}}). Personal consumption expenditures, or \textbf{consumer spending}, totals \$22.0 trillion in April 2026, or \$64,140 per person (see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}). \textbf{Personal saving}, calculated as after-tax income minus consumer spending and other outlays such as interest payments, totals \$0.61 trillion, or \$1,785 per person (see {\color{tealgreen}\textbf{---}}).
