In the national accounts, disposable personal income, or \textbf{after-tax income}, totals \$24.2 trillion, on an annualized basis, in August 2026, equivalent to \$70,620 per person (see {\color{navyblue}\textbf{---}}). Personal consumption expenditures, or \textbf{consumer spending}, totals \$22.3 trillion in August 2026, or \$65,038 per person (see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}). \textbf{Personal saving}, calculated as after-tax income minus consumer spending and other outlays such as interest payments, totals \$0.99 trillion, or \$2,887 per person (see {\color{tealgreen}\textbf{---}}).
