The \textbf{incorporated self-employed} total 7.2 million in September 2025, equivalent to 4.2 percent of the labor force (see {\color{green!80!black}\textbf{---}}). In 2019, the category made up 3.8 percent of the labor force.

Incorporated self-employed are not reported by BLS prior to 2000, but can be calculated from the CPS, and make up a average of three percent of the labor force from 1989 to 1999.
