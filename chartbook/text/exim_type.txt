In 2025 Q2, US exports of goods are equivalent to 6.8 percent of GDP while services exports total 3.9 percent of GDP. Goods imports are 10.8 percent of GDP while services imports are 2.9 percent. Imports of capital goods, excluding autos, equal 3.6 percent of GDP in 2025 Q2 and 3.3 percent of GDP one year prior.
