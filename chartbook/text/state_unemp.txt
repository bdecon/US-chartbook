In December 2025, all 50 states and DC had unemployment rates below eight percent, and the unemployment rate was above five percent in five states. Three months prior, in September 2025, no states had an unemployment rate above eight percent, and five states were above five percent. In the peak of the COVID-19 pandemic shutdowns, in April 2020, the unemployment rate was above eight percent in 48 states, and above five percent in every state.

The states with the highest unemployment rates in December 2025 are the District of Columbia (6.4 percent), Washington (5.3 percent), and Oregon (5.1 percent). The states with the lowest unemployment rates are Hawaii (2.2 percent), Alabama (2.3 percent), and South Dakota (2.4 percent).
