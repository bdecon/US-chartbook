From 2019 to 2025, aggregate pre-tax personal income increased by a total of 8.58 percent, after adjusting for changes in prices. Earnings contributed 5.44 percentage points to this change, followed by property income (1.74 percentage points), Social Security (1.16 percentage points), and Veterans' benefits (0.32 percentage point). Pension income subtracted 0.44 percentage point. The increase in earnings reflects both a larger working population and higher average earnings, which increased by a total of 3.7 percent.
