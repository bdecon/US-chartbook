Combined \textbf{assets of private nonfinancial businesses} are valued at \$92.1 trillion in the first quarter of 2025, which is 307.3 percent of GDP. These include financial and nonfinancial assets. Financial assets include cash and deposits, equity in other businesses, trade receivables, and other financial assets, and total \$42.5 trillion. Nonfinancial, or tangible, assets include real estate, equipment, inventories, and intellectual property products, and total \$49.6 trillion.
