In September 2025, there were 23.6 million \textbf{government jobs}, equivalent to 6.9 for every 100 people (see {\color{blue!50!cyan}\textbf{---}}). The previous year, in September 2024, there were 23.5 million government jobs, equivalent to 6.9 per 100 people. During the 1990s, there were 7.3 government jobs per 100 people. If the rate was the same today, there would be 1.4 million additional government jobs.

By level of government, there were 15.2 million local government jobs in September 2025, equivalent to 4.4 per 100 people (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}). In the same period, there were 5.5 million state government jobs (1.6 per 100 people, see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}), and 2.9 million federal government jobs (0.9 per 100 people, see {\color{green!80!blue}\textbf{---}}).

Since 2019, the US has gained 872,000 total government jobs. During the same period, local governments added 543,000 jobs, state governments added 248,000 jobs, while the federal government added 81,000 jobs.
