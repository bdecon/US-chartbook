In May 2026, establishment data show employees on private nonfarm payrolls (see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}) worked 34.3 hours per week on average, in line with the 34.4 average weekly hours during 2017 to 2019.

The average hours for all jobs (see \tcolorline{red!80!white}) is 32.9 per week in 2026 Q1, following 32.8 in 2025 Q1. In 1989, 34.3 hours were worked per week per job.
