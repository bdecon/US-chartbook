In August 2025, establishment data show employees on private nonfarm payrolls (see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}) worked 34.2 hours per week on average, slightly below the 34.4 average weekly hours during 2017 to 2019.

The average hours for all jobs (see \tcolorline{red!80!white}) is 32.8 per week in 2025 Q2, following 32.8 in 2024 Q2. In 1989, 34.3 hours are worked per week per job.
