Federal Reserve Bank of New York (FRBNY) \href{https://www.newyorkfed.org/microeconomics/hhdc/background.html}{data} show \$18.8 trillion in \textbf{household debt} in the fourth quarter of 2025, which translates to 81.0 percent of disposable personal income. Over the past six years, household debt increased by \$4.63 trillion, compared to a \$6.81 trillion increase in disposable personal income. As a result, the ratio of debt to income dropped by 5.4 percentage points over this period.
