Federal Reserve Bank of New York (FRBNY) \href{https://www.newyorkfed.org/microeconomics/hhdc/background.html}{data} show \$18.8 trillion in \textbf{household debt} in the first quarter of 2026, which translates to 79.9 percent of disposable personal income. Over the past six years, household debt increased by \$4.49 trillion, compared to a \$6.99 trillion increase in disposable personal income. As a result, the ratio of debt to income dropped by 6.6 percentage points over this period.
