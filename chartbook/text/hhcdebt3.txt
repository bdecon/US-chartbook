Federal Reserve Bank of New York (FRBNY) \href{https://www.newyorkfed.org/microeconomics/hhdc/background.html}{data} show \$18.2 trillion in \textbf{household debt} in the first quarter of 2025, which translates to 81.9 percent of disposable personal income. Over the past five years, household debt increased by \$3.90 trillion, compared to a \$5.71 trillion increase in disposable personal income. As a result, the ratio of debt to income dropped by 4.7 percentage points over this period.
