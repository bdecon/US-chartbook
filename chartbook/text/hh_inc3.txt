The price-adjusted 90th percentile income limit is \$234,900 in 2023 (see {\color{teal}\textbf{---}}), \$224,500 in 2022, \$237,400 in 2021, and \$186,800 in 2000. Ten percent of households make more than this level. 

On the opposite end of the income distribution, the 10th percentile income limit is \$18,980 in 2023 (see {\color{orange!80!yellow}\textbf{---}}), \$17,780 in 2022, \$17,540 in 2021, and \$17,650 in 2000. Ten percent of households make less than this level.
