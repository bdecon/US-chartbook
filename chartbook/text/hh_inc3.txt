The price-adjusted 90th percentile income limit is \$251,000 in 2024 (see {\color{teal}\textbf{---}}), \$241,000 in 2023, \$230,200 in 2022, and \$191,500 in 2000. Ten percent of households make more than this level. 

On the opposite end of the income distribution, the 10th percentile income limit is \$19,900 in 2024 (see {\color{amber}\textbf{---}}), \$19,470 in 2023, \$18,230 in 2022, and \$18,090 in 2000. Ten percent of households make less than this level.
