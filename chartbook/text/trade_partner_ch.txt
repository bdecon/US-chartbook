Over the year ending May 2025, nominal total trade increased among 19 of the top 25 trading partners. The largest one-year increase in total trade was with Taiwan. Monthly trade with Taiwan grew by 8.5 billion, or 67.4 percent. The largest one-year decrease is with China, with monthly trade falling by 19.3 billion, which is a drop of 41.6 percent. Total trade with all countries grew 2.6 percent over the year.
