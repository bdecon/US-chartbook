Over the year ending August 2026, nominal total trade increased among 23 of the top 25 trading partners. The largest one-year increase in total trade was with Mexico. Monthly trade with Mexico rose by \$19.9 billion, or 26.8 percent. The largest one-year decrease is with Ireland, with monthly trade falling by \$1.3 billion, which is a drop of 18.2 percent. Total trade with all countries grew 22.4 percent over the year.
