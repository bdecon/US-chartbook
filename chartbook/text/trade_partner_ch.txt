Over the year ending April 2026, nominal total trade increased among 14 of the top 25 trading partners. The largest one-year increase in total trade was with Mexico. Monthly trade with Mexico rose by \$16.4 billion, or 23.6 percent. The largest one-year decrease is with Ireland, with monthly trade falling by \$5.8 billion, which is a drop of 47.4 percent. Total trade with all countries grew 11.5 percent over the year.
