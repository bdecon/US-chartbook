Over the year ending June 2025, nominal total trade increased among 17 of the top 25 trading partners. The largest one-year increase in total trade was with Taiwan. Monthly trade with Taiwan grew by 7.4 billion, or 54.0 percent. The largest one-year decrease is with China, with monthly trade falling by 17.2 billion, which is a drop of 37.7 percent. Total trade with all countries grew 1.2 percent over the year.
