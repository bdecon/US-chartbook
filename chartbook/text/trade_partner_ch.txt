Over the year ending July 2025, nominal total trade increased among 18 of the top 25 trading partners. The largest one-year increase in total trade was with Taiwan. Monthly trade with Taiwan rose by 7.5 billion, or 44.6 percent. The largest one-year decrease is with China, with monthly trade falling by 15.9 billion, which is a drop of 30.8 percent. Total trade with all countries grew 2.3 percent over the year.
