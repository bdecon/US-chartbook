Over the year ending July 2026, nominal total trade increased among 19 of the top 25 trading partners. The largest one-year increase in total trade was with Mexico. Monthly trade with Mexico rose by \$20.5 billion, or 27.6 percent. The largest one-year decrease is with Switzerland, with monthly trade falling by \$4.1 billion, which is a drop of 31.3 percent. Total trade with all countries grew 13.4 percent over the year.
