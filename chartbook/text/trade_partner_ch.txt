Over the year ending October 2025, nominal total trade increased among 15 of the top 25 trading partners. The largest one-year increase in total trade was with Taiwan. Monthly trade with Taiwan rose by \$13.0 billion, or 92.6 percent. The largest one-year decrease is with China, with monthly trade falling by \$22.0 billion, which is a drop of 40.1 percent. Total trade with all countries grew 2.4 percent over the year.
