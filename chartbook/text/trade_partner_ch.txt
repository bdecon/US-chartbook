Over the year ending November 2025, nominal total trade increased among 17 of the top 25 trading partners. The largest one-year increase in total trade was with Taiwan. Monthly trade with Taiwan rose by \$12.6 billion, or 97.2 percent. The largest one-year decrease is with China, with monthly trade falling by \$22.4 billion, which is a drop of 44.5 percent. Total trade with all countries fell 0.8 percent over the year.
