Over the year ending December 2025, nominal total trade increased among 17 of the top 25 trading partners. The largest one-year increase in total trade was with Taiwan. Monthly trade with Taiwan rose by \$15.8 billion, or 115.4 percent. The largest one-year decrease is with China, with monthly trade falling by \$20.4 billion, which is a drop of 40.8 percent. Total trade with all countries grew 2.4 percent over the year.
