Over the year ending September 2025, nominal total trade increased among 12 of the top 25 trading partners. The largest one-year increase in total trade was with Ireland. Monthly trade with Ireland rose by 9.5 billion, or 78.2 percent. The largest one-year decrease is with China, with monthly trade falling by 22.4 billion, which is a drop of 41.3 percent. Total trade with all countries grew 0.9 percent over the year.
