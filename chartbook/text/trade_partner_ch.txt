Over the year ending March 2025, nominal total trade increased among 22 of the top 25 trading partners. The largest one-year increase in total trade was with Ireland. Monthly trade with Ireland grew by 22.9 billion, or 247.2 percent. The largest one-year decrease is with China, with monthly trade falling by 1.9 billion, which is a drop of 4.4 percent. Total trade with all countries grew 21.7 percent over the year.
