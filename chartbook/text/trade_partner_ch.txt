Over the year ending January 2026, nominal total trade increased among 14 of the top 25 trading partners. The largest one-year increase in total trade was with Taiwan. Monthly trade with Taiwan rose by \$12.2 billion, or 84.3 percent. The largest one-year decrease is with China, with monthly trade falling by \$22.2 billion, which is a drop of 43.0 percent. Total trade with all countries fell 7.1 percent over the year.
