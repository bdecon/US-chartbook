To gauge the overall effect of Fed interest rate policy, the \textbf{real federal funds rate} is calculated as the effective fed funds rate minus the all-item PCE inflation rate. In August 2026, the real Fed funds rate is 0.2 percent (see {\color{blue!70!white}\textbf{---}}); the estimated rate for October is 0.1 percent.

The Federal Reserve Board \href{{{sep_url}}}{publishes} a Summary of Economic Projections, which can be used to calculate the median board-member forecast for the real Fed funds rate. The September 16, 2026 forecast is 1.8 percent in 2028 and 1.6 percent in 2029 (see \tikz[baseline=-1mm]\draw[black!90!white,ultra thick,densely dashed](0,0)--+(0.33,0);).
