To gauge the overall effect of Fed interest rate policy, the \textbf{real federal funds rate} is calculated as the effective fed funds rate minus the all-item PCE inflation rate. In July 2025, the real Fed funds rate is 1.73 percent (see {\color{blue!70!white}\textbf{---}}); the estimated rate for September is 1.42 percent.

The Federal Reserve Board \href{{{sep_url}}}{publishes} a Summary of Economic Projections, which can be used to calculate the median board-member forecast for the real Fed funds rate.
