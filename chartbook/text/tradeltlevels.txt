US goods and services imports total \$350.5 billion in May 2025, following \$350.8 billion in April (see {\color{blue!80!violet}\textbf{---}}). Imports average \$373.6 billion over the latest three months of data, and \$336.9 billion during the same months, one year prior. In 2019, monthly US imports average \$259.4 billion. For additional context, imports are equivalent to \$1,025 per capita, in the latest month.

The US exported \$279.0 billion of goods and services in May 2025, following \$290.6 billion in April (see {\color{green!80!blue}\textbf{---}}). The three-month average was \$283.5 billion in May, and \$265.7 billion one year prior. Exports were \$212.8 billion per month, on average, in 2019. In the latest month, exports are equivalent to \$816 per capita or \$1,709 per worker. 

Spending on imports exceeds payments received for exports, resulting in a trade deficit. In May, the trade deficit was \$71.5 billion, following \$60.3 billion in April (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}). Over the past three months, the average trade deficit is \$90.0 billion, compared to \$71.3 billion one year prior. In 2019, the average monthly trade deficit is \$46.6 billion.
