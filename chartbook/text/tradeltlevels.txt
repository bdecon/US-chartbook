US goods and services imports total \$419.0 billion in March 2025, following \$401.2 billion in February (see {\color{blue!80!violet}\textbf{---}}). Imports average \$407.1 billion over the latest three months of data, and \$330.2 billion during the same months, one year prior. In 2019, monthly US imports average \$258.8 billion. For additional context, imports are equivalent to \$1,226 per capita, in the latest month.

The US exported \$278.5 billion of goods and services in March 2025, following \$278.0 billion in February (see {\color{green!80!blue}\textbf{---}}). The three-month average was \$275.7 billion in March, and \$262.0 billion one year prior. Exports were \$212.2 billion per month, on average, in 2019. In the latest month, exports are equivalent to \$815 per capita or \$1,703 per worker. 

Spending on imports exceeds payments received for exports, resulting in a trade deficit. In March, the trade deficit was \$140.5 billion, following \$123.2 billion in February (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}). Over the past three months, the average trade deficit is \$131.4 billion, compared to \$68.2 billion one year prior. In 2019, the average monthly trade deficit is \$46.6 billion.
