US goods and services imports total \$348.9 billion in November 2025, following \$332.1 billion in October (see {\color{blue!80!violet}\textbf{---}}). Imports average \$341.1 billion over the latest three months of data, and \$351.5 billion during the same months, one year prior. In 2019, monthly US imports averaged \$259.4 billion. For additional context, imports are equivalent to \$1,017 per capita, in the latest month.

The US exported \$292.1 billion of goods and services in November 2025, following \$302.9 billion in October (see {\color{green!80!blue}\textbf{---}}). The three-month average was \$296.4 billion in November, and \$273.0 billion one year prior. Exports were \$212.8 billion per month, on average, in 2019. In the latest month, exports are equivalent to \$851 per capita or \$1,783 per worker. 

Spending on imports exceeds payments received for exports, resulting in a trade deficit. In November, the trade deficit was \$56.8 billion, following \$29.2 billion in October (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}). Over the past three months, the average trade deficit is \$44.7 billion, compared to \$78.5 billion one year prior. In 2019, the average monthly trade deficit was \$46.6 billion.
