US goods and services imports total \$356.6 billion in January 2026, following \$359.2 billion in December 2025 (see {\color{blue!80!violet}\textbf{---}}). Imports average \$354.2 billion over the latest three months of data, and \$374.9 billion during the same months, one year prior. In 2019, monthly US imports averaged \$259.4 billion. For additional context, imports are equivalent to \$1,041 per capita, in the latest month.

The US exported \$302.1 billion of goods and services in January 2026, following \$286.3 billion in December 2025 (see {\color{tealgreen}\textbf{---}}). The three-month average was \$293.1 billion in January, and \$273.3 billion one year prior. Exports were \$212.8 billion per month, on average, in 2019. In the latest month, exports are equivalent to \$882 per capita or \$1,853 per worker. 

Spending on imports exceeds payments received for exports, resulting in a trade deficit. In January, the trade deficit was \$54.5 billion, following \$72.9 billion in December 2025 (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}). Over the past three months, the average trade deficit is \$61.1 billion, compared to \$101.7 billion one year prior. In 2019, the average monthly trade deficit was \$46.6 billion.
