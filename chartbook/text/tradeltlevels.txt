US goods and services imports total \$357.6 billion in December 2025, following \$345.3 billion in November (see {\color{blue!80!violet}\textbf{---}}). Imports average \$344.8 billion over the latest three months of data, and \$355.5 billion during the same months, one year prior. In 2019, monthly US imports averaged \$259.4 billion. For additional context, imports are equivalent to \$1,044 per capita, in the latest month.

The US exported \$287.3 billion of goods and services in December 2025, following \$292.3 billion in November (see {\color{green!80!blue}\textbf{---}}). The three-month average was \$294.1 billion in December, and \$271.8 billion one year prior. Exports were \$212.8 billion per month, on average, in 2019. In the latest month, exports are equivalent to \$839 per capita or \$1,752 per worker. 

Spending on imports exceeds payments received for exports, resulting in a trade deficit. In December, the trade deficit was \$70.3 billion, following \$53.0 billion in November (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}). Over the past three months, the average trade deficit is \$50.7 billion, compared to \$83.6 billion one year prior. In 2019, the average monthly trade deficit was \$46.6 billion.
