US goods and services imports total \$358.8 billion in July 2025, following \$338.7 billion in June (see {\color{blue!80!violet}\textbf{---}}). Imports average \$349.7 billion over the latest three months of data, and \$343.8 billion during the same months, one year prior. In 2019, monthly US imports average \$259.4 billion. For additional context, imports are equivalent to \$1,048 per capita, in the latest month.

The US exported \$280.5 billion of goods and services in July 2025, following \$279.6 billion in June (see {\color{green!80!blue}\textbf{---}}). The three-month average was \$280.2 billion in July, and \$268.1 billion one year prior. Exports were \$212.8 billion per month, on average, in 2019. In the latest month, exports are equivalent to \$819 per capita or \$1,720 per worker. 

Spending on imports exceeds payments received for exports, resulting in a trade deficit. In July, the trade deficit was \$78.3 billion, following \$59.1 billion in June (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}). Over the past three months, the average trade deficit is \$69.5 billion, compared to \$75.7 billion one year prior. In 2019, the average monthly trade deficit is \$46.6 billion.
