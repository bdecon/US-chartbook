As of July 2025, 62.2 percent of those aged 16 and over are part of the labor force (see {\color{green!80!blue!60!black}\textbf{---}}), following 62.3 percent in June and 62.4 percent in May. Pre-pandemic, in February 2020, the rate stood at 63.3 percent.

In July 2025, 67.6 percent of men age 16 and older are in the labor force (see {\color{blue!70!black}\textbf{---}}), compared to 57.1 percent of women (see {\color{red!80!black}\textbf{---}}). Since February 2020, labor force participation has decreased 1.6 percentage points among men, and decreased 0.7 percentage point among women.
