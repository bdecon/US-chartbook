As of January 2026, 62.5 percent of those aged 16 and over are in the labor force (see {\color{green!80!blue!60!black}\textbf{---}}), following 62.4 percent in December 2025 and 62.5 percent in November 2025. The 2024 average participation rate of 62.6 percent is 1.7 percentage points above the December 2015 forecast. Pre-pandemic, in February 2020, the rate stood at 63.3 percent.

In January 2026, 67.8 percent of men age 16 and older are in the labor force (see {\color{blue!70!black}\textbf{---}}), compared to 57.5 percent of women (see {\color{red!80!black}\textbf{---}}). Since February 2020, participation has decreased 1.4 percentage points among men, and decreased 0.3 percentage point among women.
