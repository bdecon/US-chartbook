As of September 2025, 62.4 percent of those aged 16 and over are in the labor force (see {\color{green!80!blue!60!black}\textbf{---}}), following 62.3 percent in August and 62.2 percent in July. The 2024 average participation rate of 62.6 percent is 1.7 percentage points above the December 2015 forecast. Pre-pandemic, in February 2020, the rate stood at 63.3 percent.

In September 2025, 67.8 percent of men age 16 and older are in the labor force (see {\color{blue!70!black}\textbf{---}}), compared to 57.4 percent of women (see {\color{red!80!black}\textbf{---}}). Since February 2020, participation has decreased 1.4 percentage points among men, and decreased 0.4 percentage point among women.
