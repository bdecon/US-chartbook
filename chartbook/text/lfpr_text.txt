As of May 2026, 61.8 percent of those aged 16 and over are in the labor force (see {\color{forestgreen}\textbf{---}}), unchanged from April, and following 61.9 percent in March. The 2024 average participation rate of 62.6 percent is 1.7 percentage points above the December 2015 forecast. Pre-pandemic, in February 2020, the rate stood at 63.3 percent.

In May 2026, 67.2 percent of men age 16 and older are in the labor force (see {\color{navyblue}\textbf{---}}), compared to 56.9 percent of women (see {\color{darkred}\textbf{---}}). Since February 2020, participation has decreased two percentage points among men, and decreased 0.9 percentage point among women.
