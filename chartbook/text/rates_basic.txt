From the 1980s to 2021, treasury yields fell considerably. The annual yield on ten-year treasuries (see {\color{violet!75!black}\textbf{---}}) fell from 8.49 percent in 1989 to 0.65 percent in July 2020. As of June 12, 2026, ten-year treasury bonds yield 4.48 percent, an increase of 0.10 percentage point from the year prior. 

Short-term treasuries more closely track the fed funds rate. Three-month bills (see {\color{magenta!90!violet}\textbf{---}}) returned an annual rate of 8.39 percent in 1989 but paid virtually no interest from 2009 to 2016. As of June 12, 2026, three-month treasuries yield 3.78 percent, a decrease of 0.64 percentage point from the year prior.
