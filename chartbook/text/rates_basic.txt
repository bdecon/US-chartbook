From the 1980s to 2021, treasury yields fell considerably. The annual yield on ten-year treasuries (see {\color{violet!75!black}\textbf{---}}) fell from 8.49 percent in 1989 to 0.65 percent in July 2020. As of September 5, 2025, ten-year treasury bonds yield 4.10 percent, an increase of 0.19 percentage point from the year prior. 

Short-term treasuries more closely track the fed funds rate. Three-month bills (see {\color{magenta!90!violet}\textbf{---}}) return an annual rate of 8.39 percent in 1989 but pay virtually no interest from 2009 to 2016. As of September 5, 2025, three-month treasuries yield 4.07 percent, a decrease of 1.14 percentage points from the year prior.
