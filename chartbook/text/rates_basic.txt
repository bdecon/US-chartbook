From the 1980s to 2021, treasury yields fell considerably. The annual yield on ten-year treasuries (see {\color{violet!75!black}\textbf{---}}) fell from 8.49 percent in 1989 to 0.65 percent in July 2020. As of December 11, 2025, ten-year treasury bonds yield 4.14 percent, a decrease of 0.01 percentage point from the year prior. 

Short-term treasuries more closely track the fed funds rate. Three-month bills (see {\color{magenta!90!violet}\textbf{---}}) return an annual rate of 8.39 percent in 1989 but pay virtually no interest from 2009 to 2016. As of December 11, 2025, three-month treasuries yield 3.66 percent, a decrease of 0.76 percentage point from the year prior.
