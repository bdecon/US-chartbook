Families in the third wealth quartile (50th to 74.9th percentiles, mean wealth of \$432,400 in 2025), have a typical income of \$99,600 in 2025 and \$79,000 in 1989 (see {\color{tealgreen!85!black}\textbf{---}}), an increase of \$20,700 (0.6 percent per year).

Second quartile (25th to 49.9th percentile, mean wealth of \$109,100) family income increased \$12,500 (0.6 percent per year) to \$67,800 in 2025, from \$55,300 in 1989 (see {\color{amber}\textbf{---}}).

 For the bottom quarter of families by wealth (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}), typical income increased \$12,700 or 1.1 percent per year to \$39,000, over the 36 years ending 2025. The bottom quarter of families have no wealth.
