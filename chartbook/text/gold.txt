As of August 14, 2025, one troy ounce of \textbf{gold} \href{https://www.lbma.org.uk/prices-and-data/precious-metal-prices#/table}{sells} for \$3,357 (see {\color{orange!40!yellow}\textbf{---}}), compared to an average of \$2,468 one year prior. Following the great recession, the monthly average price of gold reached \$1,781 per ounce, in September 2011. In August 2025, the average monthly price reached \$3,357 per ounce.
