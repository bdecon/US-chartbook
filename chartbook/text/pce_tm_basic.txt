The trimmed-mean PCE price index increased 2.2 percent over the year ending June 2026 (see {\color{violet!60!magenta}\textbf{---}}). By excluding top and bottom categories, the trimmed-mean rate is 1.49 percentage points below the all-items PCE rate. In June 2025, the trimmed-mean inflation rate was 2.7 percent, 0.11 percentage point above the all-items rate. From 2017 to 2019, the trimmed-mean rate averaged 1.9 percent, 0.12 percentage point above the all-items rate.
