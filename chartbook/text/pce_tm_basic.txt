The trimmed-mean PCE price index increased 2.6 percent over the year ending July 2025 (see {\color{violet!60!magenta}\textbf{---}}). By excluding top and bottom categories, the trimmed-mean rate is 0.05 percentage point above the all-items PCE rate. In July 2024, the trimmed-mean inflation rate is 2.8 percent, 0.32 percentage point above the all-items rate. From 2017--2019, the average trimmed-mean rate is 1.9 percent, 0.12 percentage point above the all-items rate.
