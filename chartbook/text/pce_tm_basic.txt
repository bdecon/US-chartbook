The trimmed-mean PCE price index increased 2.8 percent over the year ending August 2025 (see {\color{violet!60!magenta}\textbf{---}}). By excluding top and bottom categories, the trimmed-mean rate is 0.01 percentage point above the all-items PCE rate.
