The trimmed-mean PCE price index increased 2.4 percent over the year ending December 2025 (see {\color{violet!60!magenta}\textbf{---}}). By excluding top and bottom categories, the trimmed-mean rate is 0.46 percentage point below the all-items PCE rate. In December 2024, the trimmed-mean inflation rate was 3.0 percent, 0.25 percentage point above the all-items rate. From 2017 to 2019, the trimmed-mean rate averaged 1.9 percent, 0.12 percentage point above the all-items rate.
