The trimmed-mean PCE price index increased 2.5 percent over the year ending March 2025 (see {\color{violet!60!magenta}\textbf{---}}). By excluding top and bottom categories, the trimmed-mean rate was 0.2 percentage point above the all-items PCE rate. In February 2025, the \textbf{trimmed-mean inflation rate} was 2.6 percent, 0.1 percentage point below the all-items rate. From 2017--2019, the average trimmed-mean rate was 1.9 percent, 0.1 percentage point above the all-items rate.
