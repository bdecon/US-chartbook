The Bureau of Economic Analysis \href{https://www.bea.gov/data/income-saving/personal-income}{report} real per capita market income growth of 0.4 percent over the year ending September 2025 (see\colordashline{blue!50!cyan}). Real per capita income after taxes and transfers increased one percent (see {\color{blue!50!black}\textbf{---}}). Since 2023, market income increased at an annual rate of 1.3 percent and after-tax income grew at an annual rate of 1.7 percent.
