The Bureau of Economic Analysis \href{https://www.bea.gov/data/income-saving/personal-income}{reports} real per capita market income grew 0.3 percent over the year ending January 2026 (see\colordashline{skyblue}). Real per capita income after taxes and transfers increased 1.4 percent (see {\color{blue!50!black}\textbf{---}}). Since 2023, market income increased at an annual rate of 1.4 percent and after-tax income grew at an annual rate of 1.8 percent.
