The Bureau of Economic Analysis \href{https://www.bea.gov/data/income-saving/personal-income}{reports} real per capita market income fell 0.2 percent over the year ending December 2025 (see\colordashline{skyblue}). Real per capita income after taxes and transfers increased 0.5 percent (see {\color{blue!50!black}\textbf{---}}). Since 2023, market income increased at an annual rate of 1.2 percent and after-tax income grew at an annual rate of 1.5 percent.
