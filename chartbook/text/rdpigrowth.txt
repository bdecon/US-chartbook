The Bureau of Economic Analysis \href{https://www.bea.gov/data/income-saving/personal-income}{report} an inflation-adjusted one-year change in after-tax income per person of 1.1 percent in May 2025, 2.0 percent in April 2025, and 1.8 percent in May 2024 (see {\color{blue!80!cyan}\textbf{---}}). Over the past year, the measure has averaged 1.4 percent. During the three years before the COVID-19 pandemic, per capita after-tax income grew at an average annual rate of 2.7 percent.
