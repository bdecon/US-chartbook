Combined state and local government expenditures total \$4.3 trillion, or 13.5 percent of GDP, in 2025 Q4. BEA has not yet released receipts data for 2025 Q4; however, in 2025 Q3, combined state and local government receipts total \$4.0 trillion, or 12.8 percent of GDP. In 2025 Q3, the combined state and local government deficit was \$242 billion or 0.8 percent of GDP.
