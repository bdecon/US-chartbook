Combined state and local government expenditures total \$4.0 trillion, or 13.4 percent of GDP, in 2025 Q1. BEA has not yet released receipts data for 2025 Q1, however, in 2024 Q4, combined state and local government receipts total \$3.9 trillion, or 13.2 percent of GDP. In 2024 Q4, the combined state and local government deficit was \$46 billion or 0.2 percent of GDP.
