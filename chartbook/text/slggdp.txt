Combined state and local government expenditures total \$4.1 trillion, or 13.4 percent of GDP, in 2025 Q2. BEA has not yet released receipts data for 2025 Q2, however, in 2025 Q1, combined state and local government receipts total \$3.9 trillion, or 13.2 percent of GDP. In 2025 Q1, the combined state and local government deficit was \$77 billion or 0.3 percent of GDP.
