Over the 12 months ending November 2025, \textbf{federal government receipts} total \$5.3 trillion, compared to \$4.9 trillion one year prior, in November 2024.

Over the past 12 months, 51 percent of receipts, totalling \$2.7 trillion, are from individual income taxes (see {\color{blue!70!black}\textbf{---}}). The remaining receipts (see {\color{blue!40!cyan}\textbf{---}}) are largely social insurance contributions (\$1.8 trillion) and corporate income taxes (\$0.5 trillion).
