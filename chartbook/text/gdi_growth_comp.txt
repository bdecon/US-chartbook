In the second quarter of 2026, real GDI increased at an annual rate of 2.6 percent, following increases of 2.5 percent in Q1 and 2.2 percent in 2025 Q4. In the latest quarter, labor income subtracted 0.70 percentage point from annualized growth, profit added 3.39 percentage points, changes in indirect tax revenue and subsidies subtracted 0.30 point, and 0.25 point went to depreciation.
