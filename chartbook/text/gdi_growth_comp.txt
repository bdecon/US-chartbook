In the second quarter of 2025, real GDI increased at an annual rate of 3.8 percent, following increases of one percent in Q1 and 3.7 percent in 2024 Q4. In the latest quarter, labor income contributed 1.47 percentage points to annualized growth, profit subtracted 0.24 percentage point, changes in indirect tax revenue and subsidies added 2.25 points, and 0.35 point went to depreciation growth.
