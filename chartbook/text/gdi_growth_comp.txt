In the third quarter of 2025, real GDI increased at an annual rate of 3.5 percent, following increases of 2.6 percent in Q2 and one percent in Q1. In the latest quarter, labor income contributed 1.09 percentage points to annualized growth, profit added 0.65 percentage point, changes in indirect tax revenue and subsidies added 0.84 point, and 0.95 point went to depreciation.
