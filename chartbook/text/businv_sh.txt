Business fixed investment encompasses structures, equipment, and intellectual property, such as software and R\&D. Annualized investment in structures is \$891 billion in 2025 Q2, representing 2.9 percent of GDP (see {\color{yellow!50!orange}\textbf{---}}). Equipment investment is \$1,643 billion or 5.4 percent of GDP (see {\color{cyan!60!white}\textbf{---}}), and intellectual property investment is \$1,698 billion, which is 5.6 percent of GDP (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}).
