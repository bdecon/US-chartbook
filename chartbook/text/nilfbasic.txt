Nonparticipants age 16 and older total 105.4 million in April 2026, and make up 38.3 percent of the age 16+ population, compared to 37.3 percent in April 2019. About half of nonparticipants, and 20.6 percent of the population, are retirees in April 2026 \seebox[navyblue]{blue!60!white}, compared to 18.2 percent in April 2019 \seebox[gray]{lightgray!80!white}.

Disability or illness keeps 5.1 percent of people out of the labor force in April 2026, compared to 5.7 percent in 2019. Students who are out of the labor force make up 5.7 percent in April 2026 and 6.1 percent in April 2019, while unpaid caregivers are 4.2 percent this April and 4.9 percent in 2019.
