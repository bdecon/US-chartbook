Nonparticipants age 16 and older total 104.1 million in December 2025, and make up 37.9 percent of the age 16+ population, compared to 37.0 percent in December 2019. About half of nonparticipants, and 20.2 percent of the population, are retirees in December 2025 \seebox[blue!70!black]{blue!60!white}, compared to 18.3 percent in December 2019 \seebox[gray]{lightgray!80!white}.

Disability or illness keeps 5.1 percent of people out of the labor force in December 2025, compared to 5.7 percent in 2019. Students who are out of the labor force make up 5.5 percent in December 2025 and 5.9 percent in December 2019, while unpaid caregivers are 4.2 percent this December and 4.7 percent in 2019.
