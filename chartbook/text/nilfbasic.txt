Nonparticipants age 16 and older total 103.0 million in September 2025, and make up 37.6 percent of the age 16+ population, compared to 36.9 percent in September 2019. About half of nonparticipants, and 19.9 percent of the population, are retirees in September 2025 \seebox[blue!70!black]{blue!60!white}, compared to 18.3 percent in September 2019 \seebox[gray]{lightgray!80!white}.

Disability or illness keeps five percent of people out of the labor force in September 2025, compared to 5.6 percent in 2019. Students who are out of the labor force make up 5.7 percent in September 2025 and 5.8 percent in September 2019, while unpaid caregivers are 4.2 percent this September and 4.8 percent in 2019.
