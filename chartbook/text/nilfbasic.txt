Nonparticipants age 16 and older total 103.0 million in August 2025, and make up 37.6 percent of the age 16+ population, compared to 36.8 percent in August 2019. About half of nonparticipants, and 19.8 percent of the population, are retirees in August 2025 \seebox[blue!70!black]{blue!60!white}, compared to 18.3 percent in August 2019 \seebox[gray]{lightgray!80!white}.

Disability or illness keeps five percent of people out of the labor force in August 2025, compared to 5.5 percent in 2019. Students who are out of the labor force make up 5.1 percent in August 2025 and 5.2 percent in August 2019, while unpaid caregivers are 4.5 percent this August and five percent in 2019.
