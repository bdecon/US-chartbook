Nonparticipants age 16 and older total 102.1 million in July 2025, and make up 37.3 percent of the age 16+ population, compared to 36.4 percent in July 2019. About half of nonparticipants, and 20 percent of the population, are retirees in July 2025 \seebox{green!80!blue!72!black}, compared to 18.1 percent in July 2019 \seebox{green!80!blue!60!lime}.

Disability or illness keeps five percent of people out of the labor force in July 2025, compared to 5.6 percent in 2019. Students who are out of the labor force make up 4.5 percent in July 2025 and 4.5 percent in July 2019, while unpaid caregivers are 4.5 percent this July and 5.2 percent in 2019.
