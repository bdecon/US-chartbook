Nonparticipants age 16 and older total 101.8 million in April 2025, and make up 37.3 percent of the age 16 or older population, compared to 37.2 percent in April 2019. About half of nonparticipants, and 19.6 percent of the population, are retirees in April 2025 (see\cbox{green!80!blue!72!black}), compared to 18.0 percent in April 2019 (see\cbox{green!80!blue!60!lime}).

Disability or illness keeps an additional 4.9 percent out of the labor force in April 2025, compared to 5.7 percent in April 2019. Students who are out of the labor force make up 5.8 percent in April 2025 and 6.1 percent in April 2019, while unpaid caregivers are 4.3 percent in April 2025 and 4.9 percent in April 2019.
