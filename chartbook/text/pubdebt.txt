In the third quarter of 2025, federal government public debt totaled \$37.6 trillion, equivalent to 121.0 percent of GDP. Of this, \$16.8 trillion, or 44.7 percent of the total, is held by private domestic investors \seebox{forestgreen}. An additional \$9.2 trillion, or 24.6 percent of the total, is held by foreign investors \seebox{orange!70!white}. The remainder is held by the Federal Reserve \seebox{blue} and various government agencies and trusts \seebox{cyan!50!white}, such as the Social Security Trust Fund.
