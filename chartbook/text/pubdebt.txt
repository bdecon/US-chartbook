In the second quarter of 2025, federal government public debt totaled \$36.2 trillion, equivalent to 118.8 percent of GDP. Of this, \$15.6 trillion, or 43.1 percent of the total, is held by private domestic investors \seebox{green!60!black}. An additional \$9.1 trillion, or 25.2 percent of the total, is held by foreign investors \seebox{orange!70!white}. The remainder is held by the Federal Reserve \seebox{blue} and various government agencies and trusts \seebox{cyan!50!white}, such as the Social Security Trust Fund.
