There are several \textbf{reasons for unemployment}. In August 2026, 3.2 million people, or 1.9 percent of the labor force, were unemployed from losing their job \seebox{red!75!orange!70!white}. An additional 0.5 percent voluntarily left a job \seebox{navyblue}. Re-entrants, people who left the labor force but are looking for a new job, comprised 1.3 percent \seebox{blue!70!teal!70!gray!62!white}. Lastly, 0.4 percent of the labor force were new entrants to the labor market, looking for their first job \seebox{purple!80!darkred}.
