Over the six years ending July 2025, nonfarm payrolls increased by a total of 8,569,000. By sector, combined government payrolls grew by 1,025,000 (see\cbox{green!85!yellow!75!black}), and private payrolls increased by a total of 7,544,000 over the six-year period. Private goods-producing industries added 582,000 jobs (see\cbox{blue!80!black}), and private service-providing industries added seven million jobs (see\cbox{cyan!80!white}).
