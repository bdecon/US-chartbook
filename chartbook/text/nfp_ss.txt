Over the six years ending August 2025, nonfarm payrolls increased by a total of 8,316,000. By sector, combined government payrolls grew by 1,011,000 \seebox{green!85!yellow!75!black}, and private payrolls increased by a total of 7,305,000 over the six-year period. Private goods-producing industries added 550,000 jobs \seebox{blue!80!black}, and private service-providing industries added 6.8 million jobs \seebox{cyan!80!white}.
