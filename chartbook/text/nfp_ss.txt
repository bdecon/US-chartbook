Over the six years ending April 2025, nonfarm payrolls increased by a total of 8,894,000. By sector, combined government payrolls grew by 987,000 (see\cbox{green!85!yellow!75!black}), and private payrolls increased by a total of 7,907,000 over the six-year period. Private goods-producing industries added 722,000 jobs (see\cbox{blue!80!black}), and private service-providing industries added 7.2 million jobs (see\cbox{cyan!80!white}).
