Among those who are unemployed in April 2026, the average (mean) \textbf{duration of unemployment} is 24.4 weeks (see {\color{darkblue}\textbf{---}}), and the typical (median) duration of unemployment is 11.0 weeks (see {\color{green!75!blue}\textbf{---}}). Over the year prior to COVID-19, ending February 2020, the average duration of unemployment was 21.7 weeks and the typical duration was 9.2 weeks.
