Nonfarm payrolls increased by 177,000 in April 2025, following 185,000 jobs added in March, and 102,000 added in February (see\cbox{blue!60!purple}). Average payroll growth was 154,700 over these three months, slightly below the average of 231,700 during the previous three months. 

 During March and April 2020, the US lost a combined 22 million jobs. Since May 2020, a total of 29.1 million jobs have been added, equivalent to 133.0 percent of those lost.

To maintain a steady payroll employment rate with population growth, the US needed to add 251,000 jobs in April 2025. Pre-pandemic, in 2019, the US was adding an average of 165,500 jobs per month.
