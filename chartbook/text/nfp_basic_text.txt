Nonfarm payrolls increased by 147,000 in June 2025, following 144,000 jobs added in May, and 158,000 added in April (see\cbox{blue!60!purple}). Average payroll growth was 149,700 over these three months, in line with the average of 111,000 during the previous three months. 

 During March and April 2020, the US lost a combined 22 million jobs. Since May 2020, a total of 29.3 million jobs have been added, equivalent to 134.0 percent of those lost.

To maintain a steady payroll employment rate with population growth, the US needed to add 251,000 jobs in June 2025. Pre-pandemic, in 2019, the US was adding an average of 165,500 jobs per month.
