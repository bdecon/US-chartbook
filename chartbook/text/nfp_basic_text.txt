Nonfarm payrolls increased by 22,000 in August 2025, following 79,000 jobs added in July, and 13,000 lost in June (see\hspace{-0.8mm}\cbox{blue!60!purple}\hspace{-0.5mm}). Average payroll growth was 29,300 over these three months, slightly below the average of 99,000 during the previous three months. 

To keep pace with population growth, the US needs to add around 250,000 jobs per month. Over the past three years, the US added an average of 173,700 jobs per month.
