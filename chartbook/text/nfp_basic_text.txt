Nonfarm payrolls increased by 50,000 in December 2025, following 56,000 jobs added in November, and 173,000 lost in October \seebox{blue!60!purple}. Average payroll growth was -22,300 over these three months, slightly below the average of 51,300 during the previous three months. 

To keep pace with population growth, the US needs to add around 250,000 jobs per month. Over the past three years, the US added an average of 144,200 jobs per month.
