Nonfarm payrolls increased by 119,000 in September 2025, following -4,000 jobs lost in August, and 72,000 added in July \seebox{blue!60!purple}. Average payroll growth was 62,300 over these three months, in line with the average of 54,700 during the previous three months. 

To keep pace with population growth, the US needs to add around 250,000 jobs per month. Over the past three years, the US added an average of 169,800 jobs per month.
