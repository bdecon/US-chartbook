Of this, corporate debt is equivalent to 47.1 percent of GDP (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}). In 2019, corporate liabilities are 109.6 percent of GDP and corporate debt is 50.0 percent.

Noncorporate business sector liabilities are equivalent to 42.5 percent of GDP in 2023 Q2 and 39.4 percent in 2019 (see {\color{violet}\textbf{---}}). Noncorporate business debt is 26.4 percent of GDP in the latest data and 27.6 percent in 2019 (see {\color{blue!70!black!60!white}\textbf{---}}).
