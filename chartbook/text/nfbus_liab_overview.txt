Corporate liabilities total \$31.9 trillion in the first quarter of 2026, equivalent to 100.3 percent of GDP (see {\colordashline{red!60!orange}}). Of this, corporate debt is equivalent to 45.5 percent of GDP (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}). In 2019, corporate liabilities were 111.2 percent of GDP and corporate debt was 49.6 percent.

Noncorporate business sector liabilities are equivalent to 43.1 percent of GDP in the first quarter of 2026 and 39.0 percent in 2019 (see {\colordashline{violet}}). Noncorporate business debt is 25.6 percent of GDP in the latest data and 27.3 percent in 2019 (see {\color{navyblue!60!white}\textbf{---}}).
