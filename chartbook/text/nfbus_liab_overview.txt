Corporate liabilities total \$30.7 trillion in the third quarter of 2025, equivalent to 98.8 percent of GDP (see {\colordashline{red!60!orange}}). Of this, corporate debt is equivalent to 45.2 percent of GDP (see {\color{red}\textbf{---}}). In 2019, corporate liabilities were 109.6 percent of GDP and corporate debt was 49.8 percent.

Noncorporate business sector liabilities are equivalent to 41.6 percent of GDP in the third quarter of 2025 and 39.4 percent in 2019 (see {\colordashline{violet}}). Noncorporate business debt is 25.6 percent of GDP in the latest data and 27.6 percent in 2019 (see {\color{navyblue!60!white}\textbf{---}}).
