According to the US Financial Accounts produced by the Federal Reserve, the market value of \textbf{household and nonprofit assets} is \$190.2 trillion in 2024 Q4, equivalent to 640 percent--or 6.4 years--of GDP. Of this, \$61.3 trillion, or 32.2 percent of the total, are tangible (non-financial) assets and \$128.9 trillion, or 67.8 percent, are financial assets.

Tangible assets include peoples' homes as well as consumer durable goods, such as cars, furniture, and appliances. Owner-occupied real estate is valued at \$48.1 trillion in 2024 Q4, equivalent to 162 percent of GDP (see\cbox{green!60!teal}). The replacement value of consumer durable goods is \$8.2 trillion, or 27 percent of GDP.

Financial assets include equity in businesses--corporate and non-corporate--with a market value of \$71.6 trillion, or 241 percent of GDP (see\cbox{blue!65!black}), in 2024 Q4. Debt securities and loan assets total \$12.8 trillion, or 43 percent of GDP (see\cbox{blue!55!cyan}). Cash and deposits, including money market accounts, total \$19.4 trillion, or 65 percent of GDP (see\cbox{cyan!40!white}). Other financial assets total \$25.0 trillion.
