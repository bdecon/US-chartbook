Combined government liabilities total \$44.7 trillion in 2026 Q1, equivalent to 140.6 percent of GDP (see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}). Liabilities were 141.2 percent of GDP one year prior, in 2025 Q1, and 137.6 percent in 2019. 

 Government assets, excluding public land, are valued at \$33.9 trillion in 2026 Q1, or 106.7 percent of GDP (see {\color{blue!80!lightcyan}\textbf{---}}). Assets were 104.4 percent of GDP one year prior, and 102.6 percent in 2019.

Public wealth is government assets minus liabilities, and is equivalent to negative 33.9 percent of GDP in the latest data (see {\color{gray}\textbf{---}}).
