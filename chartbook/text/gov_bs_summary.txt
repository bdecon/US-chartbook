Combined government liabilities total \$44.9 trillion in 2026 Q2, equivalent to 138.2 percent of GDP (see {\color{orange}\textbf{---}}). Liabilities were 139.0 percent of GDP one year prior, in 2025 Q2, and 137.6 percent in 2019. 

 Government assets, excluding public land, are valued at \$34.5 trillion in 2026 Q2, or 106.2 percent of GDP (see {\color{blue!80!lightcyan}\textbf{---}}). Assets were 104.3 percent of GDP one year prior, and 102.6 percent in 2019.

Public wealth is government assets minus liabilities, and is equivalent to negative 32.0 percent of GDP in the latest data (see {\color{gray}\textbf{---}}).
