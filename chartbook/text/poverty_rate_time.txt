Since 1989, the official poverty measure (see {\color{green!50!teal!80!white}\textbf{---}}) shows between 10.5 percent and 15.1 percent of people in poverty each year, with an average poverty rate of 12.8 percent during the period. Poverty rates were above average after the recession of 1991 and after the Great Recession, and below average around 2000.
