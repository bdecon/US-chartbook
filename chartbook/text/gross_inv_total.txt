In the first quarter of 2025, annualized US \textbf{gross fixed investment}, both public and private, totals \$6.5 trillion, or 21.7 percent of GDP (see {\color{black}\textbf{---}}). Gross fixed investment is equivalent to 21.6 percent of GDP one year prior, in 2024 Q1, and averages 21.3 percent of GDP in 2019.
