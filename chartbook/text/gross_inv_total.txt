In the fourth quarter of 2025, annualized US \textbf{gross fixed investment}, both public and private, totals \$6.7 trillion, or 21.2 percent of GDP (see {\color{black}\textbf{---}}). Gross fixed investment is equivalent to 21.2 percent of GDP one year prior, in 2024 Q4, and averages 21.3 percent of GDP in 2019.
