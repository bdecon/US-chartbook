In September 2025, housing prices contributed 1.7 percentage points to the CPI one-year inflation rate of 3.0 percent, slightly below the category's September 2024 contribution of 1.8 percentage points. 

Food prices added 0.4 percentage point to September 2025 inflation, slightly above the year-prior contribution of 0.3 percentage point. Transportation prices increased the inflation rate by 0.3 percentage point in the latest data, compared to a subtraction of 0.2 percentage point in September 2024.

Medical care prices increased the inflation rate by 0.3 percentage point in September 2025, in line with the year-prior contribution of 0.3 percentage point. Energy prices make up 6.2 percent of the CPI basket and contributed 0.2 percentage point to overall inflation in the latest data, substantially above a subtraction of 0.4 percentage point one year prior.
