Public administration has the highest rate, at 32.8 percent as of September 2025, followed by education and health with 16.4 percent, and construction and mining with 10.7 percent. 

The manufacturing industry experienced the largest drop in union membership over the past 30 years, and is currently 13.8 percentage points below its February 1989 rate of 23.5 percent.

The lowest union membership rate is in finance and business services (3.4 percent). The union membership rate of the industry was 7.2 percent in March 1992.
