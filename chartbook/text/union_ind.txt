The manufacturing industry experienced the largest drop in union membership over the past 30 years, and is currently 14.7 percentage points below its February 1989 rate of 23.5 percent.

The lowest union membership rate is in finance and business services (3.3 percent). The union membership rate of the industry was 7.2 percent in March 1992.
