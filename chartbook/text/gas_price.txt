On March 9, 2026, the US average \href{https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/pet_pri_gnd_dcus_nus_w.htm}{price} for a gallon of \textbf{gasoline} is \$3.63 (see {\color{blue!80!white}\textbf{---}}), the highest level since May 2024, an increase of \$0.48 from the week prior. This gas price measure, which is the average across formulations, grades, and locations, was \$3.20 one year prior, and averaged \$2.69 in 2019. During 2011--2013, the average gas price was \$3.61.
