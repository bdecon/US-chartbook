One measure of real interest rates comes from Treasury inflation-indexed securities. The yield on these securities can be a proxy for the interest rate investors would charge for treasuries, without inflation. 

As of May 15, 2025, the real yield on ten-year treasuries is 2.11 percent (see {\color{green!85!blue}\textbf{---}}), compared to 2.08 percent three months prior, on February 11. Five-year treasuries yield 1.66 percent in the latest data, and 1.73 percent three months prior, after adjusting for expected inflation (see {\color{violet!50!blue!60!white}\textbf{---}}).
