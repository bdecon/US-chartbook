As of December 11, 2025, the average 30-year mortgage rate is 6.22 percent, compared to 6.24 percent in November 2025, and 6.71 percent in December 2024 (see {\color{blue!60!black}\textbf{---}}). In 2021, the average rate was 2.96 percent.

Mortgage rates tend to move with other long-term interest rates and with the premiums charged by primary lenders \seebox{yellow!50!brown!50!white} and mortgage-backed securities \seebox{green!60!black!85!white}. Since 1989, the spread between 30-year mortgages and 10-year treasuries averages 1.77 percentage points (see {\color{blue!60!cyan}\textbf{---}}). As of December 11, 2025, the spread is 2.08 percentage points.
