In April 2025, 80.7 percent of 25 to 54 years olds were employed (see {\color{blue!90!cyan}\textbf{---}}), the highest level since January 2025. Over the past year, the age 25 to 54 employment rate was virtually unchanged. The April 2025 rate is 0.7 percentage point (equivalent to 970,000 workers) below the average rate of 81.4 during the tight labor market of 1999--2000.
