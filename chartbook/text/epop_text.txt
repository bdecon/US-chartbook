In January 2026, 80.9 percent of 25 to 54 year-olds were employed (see {\color{blue!90!cyan}\textbf{---}}), the highest level since September 2024. Over the past year, the age 25 to 54 employment rate increased 0.2 percentage point. The January 2026 rate is 0.5 percentage point (equivalent to 710,000 workers) below the average rate of 81.4 during the tight labor market of 1999--2000.
