In July 2025, 80.4 percent of 25 to 54 years olds were employed (see {\color{blue!90!cyan}\textbf{---}}), compared to 80.7 percent in June 2025. Over the past year, the age 25 to 54 employment rate decreased 0.5 percentage point. The July 2025 rate is one percentage point (equivalent to 1.4 million workers) below the average rate of 81.4 during the tight labor market of 1999--2000.
