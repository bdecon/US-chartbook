In August 2026, 80.4 percent of 25- to 54-year-olds were employed (see {\color{blue!90!cyan}\textbf{---}}), compared to 80.4 percent in July 2026. Over the past year, the age 25 to 54 employment rate decreased 0.3 percentage point. The August 2026 rate is one percentage point (equivalent to 1.4 million workers) below the average rate of 81.4 during the tight labor market of 1999--2000.
