In September 2026, 80.7 percent of 25- to 54-year-olds were employed (see {\color{blue!90!cyan}\textbf{---}}), compared to 80.4 percent in August 2026. Over the past year, the age 25 to 54 employment rate was virtually unchanged. The September 2026 rate is 0.7 percentage point (equivalent to 970,000 workers) below the average rate of 81.4 during the tight labor market of 1999--2000.
