In 2026 Q1, credit card delinquency (see {\color{amber}\textbf{---}}) is 13.1 percent, the highest level since 2011 and up from 8.3 percent in 2019.

Student loan delinquency (see {\color{cyan!80!teal}\textbf{---}}) fell to near zero during the 2020 to 2024 federal payment pause, then returned to 10.3 percent by 2026 Q1 as missed payments are again reported to credit bureaus.

Auto loan delinquency (see {\color{navyblue}\textbf{---}}) is 5.6 percent in 2026 Q1, a record in over two decades of data.
